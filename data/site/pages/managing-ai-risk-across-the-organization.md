# Managing AI Risk Across the Organization

InnovateUS workshop · 16 December 2026 · 60 minutes · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

This session explores how agencies can develop governance processes, escalation pathways, staff guidance, and cross-functional partnerships to support safer, more responsible AI adoption. Participants will learn how different roles across an organization can contribute to identifying, communicating, and managing AI-related risks.

## Learning goals

By the end of this workshop, participants will be able to:

- **Identify** how different roles and teams across an agency contribute to managing AI-related cybersecurity risks.
- **Develop** practical approaches to staff guidance, governance, and escalation when AI-related risks arise.
- **Strengthen** cross-functional collaboration to support safer and more responsible AI adoption across the organization.
