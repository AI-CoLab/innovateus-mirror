# HR Policy, Labor Partnership, and Workforce Transitions

InnovateUS workshop · 8 February 2027 · 60 minutes · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

HR performance is defined by policies, partnerships, and the organization’s capability to make critical transitions toward new ways of doing things that reflect current priorities and values.  This session explores how agencies can develop HR policies for responsible AI use that are consistent with merit principles and civil service rules, and why unions and employees need to be partners in those decisions. It also looks at how AI can help agencies plan for succession and capture institutional knowledge when experienced employees retire or move on.  And it examines how AI enables talented employees to develop in ways that reflect shared goals.

## Learning goals

By the end of this workshop, participants will be able to:

- Identify core elements of a responsible AI use policy for HR that aligns with merit principles and civil service rules.

- Explain why involving unions and employees early in AI decisions matters, and name at least one way to start that conversation.

- Describe how AI can support succession planning and help capture institutional knowledge before experienced employees leave.
