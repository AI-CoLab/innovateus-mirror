# Testing, Improving, and Sharing What Works

InnovateUS workshop · 19 March 2027 · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

Bring together what you've learned throughout the series to test and refine an AI-supported workflow. Learn how to compare results with your current practices, assess quality and reliability, address limitations, and share effective approaches with colleagues.

Practical outcome: Leave with a tested workflow or implementation plan, including evaluation criteria and next steps for applying it in your work.

Part of the series [The Prompting Lab: Integrating AI into Your Public Sector Work](https://innovate-us.org/workshop-series/the-prompting-lab-series).
