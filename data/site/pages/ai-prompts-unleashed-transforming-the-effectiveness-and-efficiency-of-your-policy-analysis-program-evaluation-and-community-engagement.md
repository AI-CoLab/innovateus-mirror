# AI Prompts Unleashed: Transforming the Effectiveness and Efficiency of Your Policy Analysis, Program Evaluation, and Community Engagement

InnovateUS workshop · 8 May 2025 · 60 minutes

Join Dr. Deborah D. Stine for a practical, hands-on webinar designed to empower state and local government staff in policy analysis, program evaluation, and related community engagement with the latest advancements in AI-driven prompts. Participants
will learn how to craft effective AI prompts tailored to their specific needs, interactive exercises to practice prompt creation, and actional strategies on integrating AI tools into their day-to-day activities. By the end of the session, attendees will be equipped to
boost their efficiency and effectiveness, ultimately leading to more impactful societal outcomes.

## Learning goals

- Develop expertise in crafting tailored AI prompts that address specific state and local government policy analysis and program evaluation needs
- Apply practical prompt creation techniques through interactive exercises designed to enhance community engagement and government service delivery
- Implement actionable strategies for integrating AI tools into daily workflows to increase efficiency and drive more impactful societal outcomes
