# Getting Started Without Getting Overwhelmed - How procurement teams are taking their first AI steps

InnovateUS workshop · 28 September 2026 · 60 minutes

Learn how procurement teams are taking their first steps with AI without a big budget or a dedicated tech team. Two practitioner stories show how a "start small" approach can turn a routine, time-consuming task into a quick, reliable one, and how a small, resourceful team found its own starting point without extra resources. Walk away with a practical way to identify where AI can support your team's work first.

## Learning goals

- Identify a small, low-risk task in your own procurement process where AI could save time or reduce error

- Recognize what conditions (data, tools, staff buy-in) make a first AI project more likely to succeed

- Apply a simple framework for scoping and piloting a first AI use case in your organization
