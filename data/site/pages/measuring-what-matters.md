# Measuring What Matters

InnovateUS workshop · 15 September 2026 · 60 minutes

Many AI evaluations focus on efficiency metrics such as time saved, documents processed, or staff hours reduced. This session examines how agencies can move beyond productivity measures to define success in terms of service quality, accuracy, equity, public trust, resident and worker experience, and public outcomes. Participants will learn practical approaches for identifying meaningful measures of public value.

## Learning goals

By the end of this workshop, participants will be able to:

- **Distinguish** between efficiency metrics and broader measures of AI effectiveness and public value.
- **Identify** meaningful measures of success across service quality, accuracy, equity, public trust, and resident and worker experience.
- **Develop** practical measures for evaluating whether AI use is improving public services and contributing to better outcomes.
