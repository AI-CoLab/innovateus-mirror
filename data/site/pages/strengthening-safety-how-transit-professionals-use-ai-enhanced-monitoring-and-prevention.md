# Strengthening Safety: How Transit Professionals Use AI-Enhanced Monitoring and Prevention

InnovateUS workshop

This session examines how safety professionals use AI-assisted systems to identify risks earlier, respond faster, and monitor conditions—while maintaining human oversight. Participants explore real-world applications, safeguards, and strategies for building public trust and protecting privacy.
