# Making Sense of AI for Public Transportation: A Practical Introduction for Transit Teams

InnovateUS workshop

This introductory session demystifies AI for transit professionals by focusing on what teams can actually do with emerging tools and how to evaluate where they add value. Participants explore real transit examples, learn how AI can support decision-making, and discuss the skills and organizational practices needed for safe, effective adoption.
