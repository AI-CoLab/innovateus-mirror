# Human-Centered Design Series: Introduction to Human-Centered Design for the Public Sector

InnovateUS workshop · 29 November 2023 · 90 minutes

In this 90-minute virtual training on Human-Centered Design, participants will learn how to use design thinking for public problem-solving. Participants will be given an overview of the five key phases of the human-centered design process and will learn strategies for using human-centered design to improve government services for those who most need them.

## Learning goals

- Learn how to use design thinking for problem-solving
- Learn about the five key phases of the human-centered design process
- Learn strategies for using human-centered design to improve government services for those who most need them.
