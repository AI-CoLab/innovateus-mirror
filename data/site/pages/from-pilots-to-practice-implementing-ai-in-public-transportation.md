# From Pilots to Practice: Implementing AI in Public Transportation

InnovateUS workshop · 18 March 2027 · 60 minutes · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

Moving from promising AI applications to improvements in public transportation requires careful planning, collaboration, and evaluation. Learn how to identify suitable use cases, assess organizational readiness, and make informed decisions about AI tools and vendors. Explore practical approaches to testing AI applications, engaging frontline staff and passengers, managing implementation risks, and measuring whether AI-supported initiatives deliver meaningful improvements in public transportation.

Part of the series [AI in Public Transportation](https://innovate-us.org/workshop-series/ai-in-public-transportation).
