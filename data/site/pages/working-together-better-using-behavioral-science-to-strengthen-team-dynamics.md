# Working Together, Better: Using Behavioral Science to Strengthen Team Dynamics

InnovateUS workshop · 28 May 2025 · 60 minutes

When people work effectively in teams, they can get more done and feel more engaged. Fundamentally, the question of how people work with other people is a question of human behavior, and so “behavioral science” is a key lens organizations can use to understand and improve team dynamics. Join us for this workshop with Erin Britton and Jonathan Vayness, and learn simple tools that can help you better understand what makes a team strong.

## Learning goals

- Better understand what makes a team strong, and why

- Create lasting environments where teams can thrive

- Consistently get the best ideas out of team members
