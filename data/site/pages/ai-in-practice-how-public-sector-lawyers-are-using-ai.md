# AI in Practice: How Public-Sector Lawyers Are Using AI

InnovateUS workshop · 14 October 2026 · 60 minutes

How are government attorneys and legal staff actually using AI today? This session explores practical applications across legal research, drafting, policy analysis, procurement, compliance, public guidance, investigations, discovery, and document review. Participants will examine real-world examples, lessons learned, and strategies for identifying appropriate use cases within their own organizations.
