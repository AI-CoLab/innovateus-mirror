# Smarter Transit: Using AI for Planning and Operations

InnovateUS workshop · 16 March 2027 · 60 minutes · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

How can public transportation agencies use AI to deliver more reliable and responsive services? Explore practical applications of AI for predicting passenger demand, optimizing routes and schedules, managing fleets, and anticipating maintenance needs. Drawing on real-world examples, learn how to identify opportunities to use AI in transit operations and planning, assess data requirements, and evaluate potential benefits and limitations.

Part of the series [AI in Public Transportation](https://innovate-us.org/workshop-series/ai-in-public-transportation).
