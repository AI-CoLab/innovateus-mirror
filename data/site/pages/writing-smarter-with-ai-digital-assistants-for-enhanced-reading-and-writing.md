# Writing Smarter with AI: Digital Assistants for Enhanced Reading and Writing

InnovateUS workshop · 22 January 2025 · 60 minutes

AI writing tools have advanced far beyond simple spelling and grammar correction, now offering personalized feedback, real-time insights, and curriculum-aligned resources. These digital assistants can provide dynamic, interactive support to strengthen reading and writing comprehension through adaptive learning experiences. In this workshop, participants will: 1) discover the evolution of AI writing assistants and the latest trends shaping the field, 2) explore key research questions and future directions for digital writing assistants, 3) dive deep into popular AI writing tools to analyze their potential for personalized feedback and student support, and 4) engage in a hands-on activity designed to demonstrate how AI tools can empower educators to assess writing skills, identify areas for growth, and foster stronger comprehension.

## Learning goals

- Discover the evolution of AI writing assistants and the latest trends shaping the field
- Explore key research questions and future directions for digital writing assistants
- Dive deep into popular AI writing tools to analyze their potential for personalized feedback and student support
- Engage in a hands-on activity designed to demonstrate how AI tools can empower educators to assess writing skills, identify areas for growth, and foster stronger comprehension.
