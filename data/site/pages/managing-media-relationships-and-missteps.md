# Managing Media Relationships and Missteps

InnovateUS workshop · 5 March 2026 · 60 minutes

Building strong relationships with reporters is a long-term investment for government communicators, but even the best-managed relationships can face challenges. This session examines how to cultivate trust with the press, respond constructively when errors occur, and correct the record without escalating tensions. Participants will come away with strategies for managing the inevitable missteps of public communication while protecting institutional credibility.
