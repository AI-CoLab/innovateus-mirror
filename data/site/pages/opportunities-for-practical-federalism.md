# Opportunities for Practical Federalism

InnovateUS workshop · 20 March 2025 · 60 minutes

In this session, participants will explore the complex interplay between federal and state governance, examine common challenges in implementing federal programs at the state level, and learn effective strategies for maximizing state autonomy while maintaining compliance with federal requirements. The workshop will cover practical topics such as federal grant management, opportunities for state policy innovation within federal frameworks, and techniques for productive engagement with federal agencies. Attendees will gain actionable insights into leveraging federal resources while preserving state interests, understanding key Supreme Court decisions affecting state authority, and identifying opportunities for policy leadership within the federal system.

## Learning goals

- Differentiate between exclusive federal powers, state powers, and shared authorities to identify opportunities for greater autonomy and policy innovation while maintaining compliance with federal requirements and achieving program objectives

- Apply lessons from successful intergovernmental partnerships to improve collaboration with federal agencies, focusing on effective communication strategies, negotiation approaches, and relationship-building techniques
