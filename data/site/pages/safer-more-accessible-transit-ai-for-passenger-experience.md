# Safer, More Accessible Transit: AI for Passenger Experience

InnovateUS workshop · 17 March 2027 · 60 minutes · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

Public transportation must be safe, accessible, and responsive to the needs of different passengers. Learn how transit agencies can use AI to support safety monitoring, identify service disruptions, provide real-time passenger information, and improve accessibility. Through practical examples, explore how to assess these applications while addressing privacy, fairness, and the importance of maintaining human oversight.

Part of the series [AI in Public Transportation](https://innovate-us.org/workshop-series/ai-in-public-transportation).
