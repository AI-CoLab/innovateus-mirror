# Thinking Like a Reporter — What Makes News

InnovateUS workshop · 9 December 2025 · 60 minutes

Understanding how journalists evaluate stories and sources is essential for effective public communication. In this session, participants will gain insight into newsroom logic—what makes a story newsworthy, how reporters assess credibility, and why timing matters. They will leave better prepared to anticipate journalistic questions, pitch stories effectively, and build relationships with the press based on transparency and trust.

## Learning goals

**Learning Goals**

 1. **Understand how journalists make decisions** about newsworthiness, credibility, timing, and sourcing—and what this means for public-sector communication.

 2. **Anticipate and respond to common journalistic questions** by applying core newsroom logic to your own messaging and outreach.

 3. **Develop strategies to pitch stories and engage with reporters** in ways that foster transparency, trust, and stronger long-term press relationships.
