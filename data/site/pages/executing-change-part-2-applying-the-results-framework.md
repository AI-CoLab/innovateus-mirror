# Executing Change Part 2: Applying the Results Framework

InnovateUS workshop · 18 February 2025 · 90 minutes

Join us for a 90-minute, interactive, virtual training with Steve Kadish, former Chief of Staff to former Massachusetts Governor Charlie Baker. Together, Kadish (Democrat) and Governor Baker (Republican) developed the Results Framework to focus on the necessary but often overlooked parts of good governing: successful execution and implementation in the delivery of public services. Their bipartisan collaboration demonstrates how effective governance transcends political boundaries when focused on practical results.

## Learning goals

- Explore real situation case studies across diverse sectors—health care, social welfare, transportation—demonstrating how the bipartisan-developed Results Framework adapts to different contexts and challenges
- Learn how to apply powerful implementation methods like agile scrum and business process analysis in public sector settings, with specific attention to government constraints and opportunities
- Gain practical knowledge on achieving strong project results, from initial setup to performance measurement, including establishing meaningful metrics, tracking progress, and making data-driven adjustments
