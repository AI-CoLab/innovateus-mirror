# Drafting and Reviewing RFPs: Using AI to Improve Clarity and Consistency

InnovateUS workshop

This session explores how AI can support the drafting and review of RFPs and other procurement documents. Participants will examine how AI-assisted tools can help improve clarity, identify inconsistencies, and align requirements with policy—while ensuring that final decisions remain firmly in human hands.
