# Equity Analysis for Public Policy and Programs

InnovateUS workshop · 10 December 2024 · 90 minutes

- Receive an understanding of equity and how to assess equity in public policy when analyzing the status of equity in current programs, changes to those programs, and potential new policies
- Hear how equity, justice, inclusion, and underserved communities are defined by federal agency equity policies, as well as community concerns about equity issues in some federal policies
- Understand equity challenges and opportunities; methods for community engagement on equity issues; and equity measurement in program evaluation and policy analysis
- Learn about available data sources and tools to assess equity as well as methods to communicate the results of an equity analysis.
