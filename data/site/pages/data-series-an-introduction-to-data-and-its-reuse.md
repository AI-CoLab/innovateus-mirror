# Data Series: An Introduction to Data and Its Reuse

InnovateUS workshop · 24 October 2023 · 60 minutes

In this 60-minute virtual training geared at demystifying data and highlighting the significance of data in the digital age, participants will receive an introduction to data and its reuse, including an overview of the different forms of data, an explanation of data reuse and why it matters, and the benefits and challenges of reusing data. Participants will explore the questions that data can provide answers to and understand the scope and limitations of data-driven decision-making. The workshop will include real-world examples of data-driven solutions and outcomes, case studies showcasing the successful reuse of data across industries, and recent developments and emerging trends in the data ecosystem.

## Learning goals

- Learn about the different forms of data
- Gain an understanding of data reuse and why it matters
- Understand the benefits and challenges of reusing data
- Explore the questions that data can provide answers to
- Understand the scope and limitations of data-driven decision-making
