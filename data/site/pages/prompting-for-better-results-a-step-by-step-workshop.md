# Prompting for Better Results: A Step-by-Step Workshop

InnovateUS workshop · 29 January 2027 · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

Practice writing, testing, and refining prompts for common public sector tasks. Learn how to provide context, structure instructions, specify output formats, and improve results through guided exercises using the AI tools available to you.

Practical outcome: Develop and test a reusable prompt for a task you perform regularly.

Part of the series [The Prompting Lab: Integrating AI into Your Public Sector Work](https://innovate-us.org/workshop-series/the-prompting-lab-series).
