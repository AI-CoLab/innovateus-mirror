# Navigating Crisis Communications with Confidence

InnovateUS workshop · 3 March 2026 · 60 minutes

Crises demand speed, clarity, and composure. This workshop focuses on best practices for communicating under pressure, with particular attention to tone, timing, and transparency. Participants will learn how to balance urgency with accuracy, maintain credibility during high-stakes moments, and prepare their organizations to respond with confidence.
