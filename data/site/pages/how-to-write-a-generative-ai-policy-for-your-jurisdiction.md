# How to Write a Generative AI Policy for Your Jurisdiction

InnovateUS workshop · 14 June 2023 · 90 minutes

Join Boston's Chief Information Officer, [Santiago Garces ](https://www.boston.gov/departments/innovation-and-technology/santiago-santi-garces)for this 90-minute virtual training, where participants will learn about how he developed the Boston guidelines on using generative AI in public service. Participants will learn about several scenarios in which government workers might want to use AI to improve how they work, as well as the process, decision-making, and recommendations involved in creating a generative AI policy for government.

## Learning goals

-  Learn about several scenarios in which government workers might want to use AI to improve how they work
- Understand the process, decision-making, and recommendations involved in creating a generative AI policy for government
