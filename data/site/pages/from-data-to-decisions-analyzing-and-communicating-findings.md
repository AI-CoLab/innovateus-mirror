# From Data to Decisions: Analyzing and Communicating Findings

InnovateUS workshop · 28 January 2027 · 60 minutes · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

Working with data ultimately means deciding what the evidence tells you and communicating it in ways that others can understand and use. Learn how to identify meaningful findings, select appropriate visualizations, and translate analysis into clear information for colleagues, decision-makers, and the public.

Practice using AI to support analysis, create visualizations, and develop initial summaries while maintaining responsibility for interpreting and verifying the results. Learn how to communicate uncertainty and limitations, avoid overstating what the data shows, and distinguish between evidence, interpretation, and recommendations.

**Tool and opportunity:** Use data and AI to develop and communicate evidence-informed findings.
**Risk and responsibility:** Ensure that conclusions accurately reflect the evidence, limitations and uncertainty are visible, and AI-assisted outputs are independently reviewed before they inform public decisions.

Part of the series [Data Literacy for Public Service:Understanding and Using Data in the AI Age](https://innovate-us.org/workshop-series/data-literacy-for-public-service-understanding-and-using-data-in-the-ai-age).
