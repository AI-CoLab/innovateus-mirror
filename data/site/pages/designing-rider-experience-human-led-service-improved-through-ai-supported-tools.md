# Designing Rider Experience: Human-Led Service Improved Through AI-Supported Tools

InnovateUS workshop

This workshop examines how transit teams use AI to enhance rider-facing services, including real-time information, multilingual communication, and accessible digital tools. The session emphasizes human-centered design, practical governance, and continuous feedback as foundations for improving rider experience.
