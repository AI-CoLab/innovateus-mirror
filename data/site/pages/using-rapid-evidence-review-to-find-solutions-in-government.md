# Using Rapid Evidence Review to Find Solutions in Government

InnovateUS workshop · 30 March 2023 · 90 minutes

Join Peter Bragge, Director of Monash Sustainable Development Institute’s [Evidence Review Service (ERS)](https://www.monash.edu/msdi/research/msdi-evidence-review-service), in this 90-minute virtual training, where participants will learn about rapid evidence review, the skill of quickly getting up to speed on existing solutions to a problem. Participants will learn how to find solutions that have been tried before in government, how to assess the success of those solutions, and better evaluate whether solutions that worked elsewhere may help them advance their own work.

## Learning goals

- Learn the skill of quickly getting up to speed on existing solutions to a problem
- Learn how to better evaluate whether solutions that worked elsewhere may help you advance in your own work
