# Human-Centered Design Series: Human-Centered Design in Government

InnovateUS workshop · 13 December 2023 · 90 minutes

In this 90-minute virtual training, participants will learn about how the human-centered design process is put into action in the public sector. This workshop will provide case studies from municipal, state, and national government. Case studies will include human-centered design projects, focusing in particular on workforce and community development. This workshop will help participants understand how the theory is applied in practice, and how human-centered design projects can be accomplished even within tight budgets.

## Learning goals

- Learn how the human-centered design process is put into action in the public sector
-  Learn from municipal, state, and national government case studies focusing in particular on workforce and community development
-  Understand how human-centered design theory is applied in practice
- Learn how human-centered design projects can be accomplished even within tight budgets.
