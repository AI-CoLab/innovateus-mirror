# Sustaining Accessibility: Governance, Accountability, and Continuous Improvement

InnovateUS workshop · 28 January 2027 · 60 minutes · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

Accessibility can decline as websites change, new documents are published, applications are updated, and responsibilities shift. This session examines how state and local agencies can incorporate accessibility into routine digital operations through monitoring, documentation, assigned responsibilities, and periodic review.

Participants will consider governance approaches ranging from simple departmental tracking to agency-wide programs. The session will examine how automated scanning and document-monitoring tools can help identify changes over time, while recognizing that a score or alert does not establish conformance by itself. It will also address how agencies can document their activities, evaluate results, and communicate progress to leadership and the public.

Part of the series [Using AI for Digital Accessibility](https://innovate-us.org/workshop-series/using-ai-for-digital-accessibility).
