# Redesigning Organizations: Moving Towards Collaborative Decision-Making

InnovateUS workshop · 18 January 2024 · 90 minutes

Join us for the 90-minute virtual training with Christian Bason, former CEO of the Danish Design Center (DDC). At DDC, Christian led the organization through a restructuring which placed the human perspective as the guiding management principle and moved towards flatter hierarchy, more self-management, and collaborative decision-making. In this training, Christian will offer insights for the public sector on better ways of working, the future of organization, and how to create conditions where employees can thrive and create positive change.

## Learning goals

- Understand better ways of working, the future of organization, and how to create conditions where employees can thrive and create positive change

- Improve collaborative decision-making
