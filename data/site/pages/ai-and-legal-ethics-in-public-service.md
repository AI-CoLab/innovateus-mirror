# AI and Legal Ethics in Public Service

InnovateUS workshop · 21 October 2026 · 60 minutes

How do professional responsibility obligations apply when AI is used in legal practice? This session explores competence, confidentiality, supervision, transparency, accountability, and the continuing role of human judgment. Participants will examine emerging guidance from bar associations, government agencies, and legal organizations and discuss how public-sector obligations may differ from private-sector practice.
