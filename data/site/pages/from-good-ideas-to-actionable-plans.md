# From Good Ideas to Actionable Plans

InnovateUS workshop · 2 February 2027 · 60 minutes · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

As a public manager, you see opportunities to improve how your team works and how services are delivered. But recognizing what needs to change is only the first step. Learn how to define the problem, set clear goals, identify where to start, and develop a realistic plan that fits your team's capacity. Through real government examples, you will learn how to break ideas into manageable steps, involve the right people, and distinguish between changes you can lead directly and those that require support from others.

## Learning goals

- Define a specific problem their team faces and frame a clear, realistic goal for improving it.

- Break an improvement idea into a small first step that fits their team’s current capacity.

- Distinguish between changes they can lead directly and those that need support from senior leaders or other parts of the organization.

Part of the series [From Ideas to Action: Leading and Implementing Change as a Public Sector Manager](https://innovate-us.org/workshop-series/from-ideas-to-action-leading-and-implementing-change-in-government).
