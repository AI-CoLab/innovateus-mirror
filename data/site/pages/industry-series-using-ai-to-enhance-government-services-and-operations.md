# Industry Series: Using AI to Enhance Government Services and Operations

InnovateUS workshop · 27 March 2024 · 60 minutes

In this 60-minute session, Microsoft’s global public sector team will share examples of how state and municipal governments and government agencies are using artificial intelligence to enhance services and operations, including traffic flow analysis, improving healthcare services, cyber-attack prevention and improving services to citizens. 

*This workshop is part of InnovateUS’s new “Artificial Intelligence Industry Series," where speakers from leading technology companies will teach participants how to use specific artificial intelligence tools. We do not promote or endorse any particular products, but we want to offer our audience a chance to learn more about using specific upcoming tools for their everyday work.*

## Learning goals

- Learn how state and municipal governments and government agencies are using artificial intelligence to enhance services and operations

- Hear case studies on how AI is being used in traffic flow analysis, improving healthcare services, cyber-attack prevention and improving services to citizens
