# Using AI for Research and Analysis

InnovateUS workshop · 26 February 2027 · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

Practice using AI to formulate research questions, organize evidence, compare sources, and synthesize information. Learn how to verify AI-generated outputs, recognize unsupported claims, and combine AI-assisted analysis with professional judgment to support public sector decisions.

Practical outcome: Prepare a short, evidence-informed analysis of a public sector question.

Part of the series [The Prompting Lab: Integrating AI into Your Public Sector Work](https://innovate-us.org/workshop-series/the-prompting-lab-series).
