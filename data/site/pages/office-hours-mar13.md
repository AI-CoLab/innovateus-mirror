# Prompting Lab Office Hours: Bring your AI Questions

InnovateUS workshop · 13 March 2026 · 45 minutes

Join Ray Bell,  AI/ML Product Director, State of Maryland, for Prompting Lab Office Hours, a live space for public sector professionals to ask questions, test ideas, and troubleshoot how generative AI fits into daily work. Bring what you’re working on and walk away with clearer, more confident next steps.

## Speakers

- **Ray Bell**, AI/ML Product Director, State of Maryland

Part of the series [The Prompting Lab: Integrating AI into Your Public Sector Work](https://innovate-us.org/workshop-series/the-prompting-lab-series).
