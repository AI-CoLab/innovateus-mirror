# Better Government for All: A Practical Introduction to Entity Resolution with AI

InnovateUS workshop · 16 January 2025 · 90 minutes

In this 90-minute session, you’ll gain a clear understanding of how entity resolution supports better governance and decision-making, while also addressing key concerns around privacy and ethics. Whether you're involved in social services, justice, healthcare, or any other area that relies on accurate data, this workshop will equip you with the insights to enhance your agency's performance.

## Learning goals

- Understand what entity resolution is and why it matters for better government services

- Explore real-world examples of how entity resolution simplifies tasks like clearing criminal records and distributing benefits

- Learn best practices for using this technology to boost efficiency in government operations

- Address ethical considerations, including privacy and data management
