# Making Change Happen: From Implementation to Lasting Improvement

InnovateUS workshop · 23 February 2027 · 60 minutes · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

Even well-planned changes face unexpected challenges once implementation begins. Learn practical approaches to testing improvements on a small scale, assigning responsibilities, tracking progress, and adapting plans based on evidence and feedback. Explore how managers can help their teams navigate setbacks, demonstrate progress to leadership, and integrate new practices into everyday work. Practice identifying meaningful measures of success and developing a plan to sustain improvements that deliver better results for the people you serve.

## Learning goals

- Design a small-scale test of an improvement, with clear owners for each part of the work.

- Identify one or two meaningful measures of success and use them to track progress and show results to leadership.

- Outline steps to adjust a plan based on feedback and setbacks, and to build the new practice into the team’s everyday work.

Part of the series [From Ideas to Action: Leading and Implementing Change as a Public Sector Manager](https://innovate-us.org/workshop-series/from-ideas-to-action-leading-and-implementing-change-in-government).
