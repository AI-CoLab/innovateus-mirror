# Mastering dX: De-risking Digital Transformation by Focusing on the "Right Problem"

InnovateUS workshop · 21 May 2024 · 45 minutes

Beth Simone Noveck and Seth Harris will explore how to best approach digital transformation in the nation’s workforce development system in a workshop hosted by NASWA. Discussion will center around the power of using data analytics and Human-Centered Design to understand areas ready for innovation as well as defining “the right problem."

## Learning goals

- Explore how to best approach digital transformation in the nation’s workforce development system
- Learn how to use data analytics and Human-Centered Design to understand areas ready for innovation as well as defining “the right problem”

## Speakers

- **Beth Simone Noveck**, Founder of InnovateUS and Director, Burnes Center for Change and the GovLab
- **Seth Harris**, Former Acting Secretary of Labor, Former Deputy Assistant to the President, Professor and Senior Fellow
