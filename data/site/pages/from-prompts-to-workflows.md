# From Prompts to Workflows

InnovateUS workshop · 30 November 2026 · 60 minutes · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

Most public professionals use AI one task at a time. Agents offer the possibility of creating reusable workflows that can support recurring work. This session explores how agencies are using agentic workflows for research, communications, permitting, grants management, procurement, case preparation, constituent services, and other operational activities. Participants will learn how to identify workflows that may benefit from agentic approaches and where simpler tools may be sufficient.
