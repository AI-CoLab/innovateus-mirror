# Finding Where AI Fits Your Work

InnovateUS workshop · 15 January 2027 · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

Learn how to identify opportunities to use AI in your daily responsibilities and distinguish between tasks where AI can add value and those where it may not be appropriate. Through guided exercises and public sector examples, identify a practical task you would like to improve.

Practical outcome: Select a real work task to develop throughout the series.

Part of the series [The Prompting Lab: Integrating AI into Your Public Sector Work](https://innovate-us.org/workshop-series/the-prompting-lab-series).
