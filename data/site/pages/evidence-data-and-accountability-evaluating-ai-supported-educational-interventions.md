# Evidence, Data, and Accountability: Evaluating AI-Supported Educational Interventions

InnovateUS workshop

This workshop focuses on how policymakers can assess whether AI-supported literacy initiatives are effective. Participants will examine evidence standards, evaluation frameworks, and data strategies that support informed decision-making and responsible public investment.
