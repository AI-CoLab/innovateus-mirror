# Evaluating Accessibility at Scale: Using AI-Assisted Tools Responsibly

InnovateUS workshop · 14 January 2027 · 60 minutes · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

Conventional accessibility scanners can identify many common technical failures, but they detect only a portion of the barriers people encounter and may also generate false positives or other noise. AI-powered testing may extend automated detection by performing more complex interactions and interpreting contextual information. Examples include navigation menus that appear only on hover and autocomplete results that cannot be navigated by keyboard.

This session examines how AI-assisted evaluations work, what evidence is available about their performance, and how agencies can assess vendor claims about improved detection. Participants will hear perspectives from accessibility professionals and tool developers about efforts to automate portions of testing that have traditionally required people. The discussion will also consider reliability, bias, false results, and the continuing role of manual testing by accessibility professionals and assistive-technology users.

Part of the series [Using AI for Digital Accessibility](https://innovate-us.org/workshop-series/using-ai-for-digital-accessibility).
