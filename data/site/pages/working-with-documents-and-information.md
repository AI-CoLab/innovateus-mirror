# Working with Documents and Information

InnovateUS workshop · 12 February 2027 · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

Learn how to use AI to summarize reports, compare documents, extract relevant information, and prepare initial drafts. Practice step by step with common government materials while learning to preserve context, verify accuracy, and protect sensitive information.

Practical outcome: Develop a reusable approach to a document-intensive task, including steps for checking results.

Part of the series [The Prompting Lab: Integrating AI into Your Public Sector Work](https://innovate-us.org/workshop-series/the-prompting-lab-series).
