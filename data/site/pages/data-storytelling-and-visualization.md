# Data Storytelling and Visualization

InnovateUS workshop · 13 November 2024 · 60 minutes

This workshop explores the art and innovation of data storytelling and visualization, focusing on transforming complex datasets into clear, compelling narratives. Led by experts from the IMF’s Creative Lab, participants will delve into the process of innovating within data visualization, learning how to harness design thinking and behavioral insights to communicate effectively with diverse audiences. Through real-world case studies, attendees will gain practical insights into how data-driven storytelling can address global and institutional challenges, bridging data with actionable insights for impactful decision-making.
