# The Rise of the Citizen Developer

InnovateUS workshop · 1 December 2026 · 60 minutes · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

AI is expanding who can help build and improve government services. Policy experts, analysts, program managers, and frontline staff can now use AI-enabled tools to prototype applications, automate routine workflows, and solve operational problems that once required specialized technical support.

In this workshop, you will explore how public agencies can support citizen developers while maintaining the governance, training, and oversight needed to innovate responsibly. You will learn how AI fellowships, internal support models, and cross-functional collaboration can help agencies turn staff expertise into practical service improvements.

## Learning goals

By the end of this workshop, participants will be able to:

- **Identify **opportunities for nontechnical staff to use AI-enabled tools to improve workflows and services.
- **Apply **governance and support practices that help citizen developers innovate responsibly.
- **Evaluate **training, fellowship, and collaboration models that build internal AI capacity.
