# New InnovateUS Fall Workshops on AI for Law Enforcement, AI for Communications Professionals, and AI for Public Engagement

By Jessica Silverman · 19 August 2025

InnovateUS is launching three new workshop series to help public sector professionals navigate critical AI challenges. Each series features practical, one-hour workshops that give you the skills to use AI more effectively in your work.

Sign up for one or all at https://innovate-us.org/workshops.

- AI for Law Enforcement
- AI for Democratic Engagement
- AI for Public Communications Professionals

## AI for Law Enforcement

Sign up:[ innovate-us.org/law-enforcement](https://innovate-us.org/law-enforcement)

AI offers significant opportunities for law enforcement—from analyzing body camera footage to improve officer training and accountability, to processing vast amounts of data for crime pattern analysis, to automating routine administrative tasks. However, these same technologies carry substantial risks of algorithmic bias, particularly in predictive policing systems that may unfairly target certain communities, or facial recognition tools that have documented higher error rates for people of color. Many agencies lack the knowledge and frameworks needed to harness AI's benefits while mitigating these risks and addressing legitimate community concerns about privacy, fairness, and accountability.

**Beginning September 4th…**.A workshop series that equips law enforcement professionals, public safety personnel, and community stakeholders with the knowledge and tools to use artificial intelligence responsibly to protect the public, reduce crime, and promote fairness.

**Hosts:** This series has been developed in collaboration with the[ Miller Center on Policing and Community Resilience](https://millercenter.rutgers.edu/) at Rutgers University and the Office of the Chief AI Strategist for the State of New Jersey.

Workshop Schedule

- Sept 10: What Can AI Actually Do for Law Enforcement Today?

- Sept 25: AI Policy Development

- Oct 1: AI in Police Operations

- Oct 6: Stakeholder Engagement in AI Implementation

- Oct 27: Predictive Policing and Algorithmic Bias

- Oct 29: Facial Recognition: Applications, Risks, and Limits

- Nov 5: Unlocking the Power of Body-Worn Camera Data with AI

**Who should attend:** Law enforcement professionals, public safety personnel, community organizations, policymakers, and anyone interested in the responsible intersection of AI and public safety.

Sign up:[ innovate-us.org/law-enforcement](https://innovate-us.org/law-enforcement)

 

## Reboot Democracy: Designing Democratic Engagement for the AI Era

Sign up:[innovate-us.org/democratic-engagement](http://innovate-us.org/democratic-engagement) 

There's no shortage of writing about the importance of citizen engagement, but far less practical guidance on how to actually do it well. Most public officials know they should engage with communities, but lack concrete skills for designing effective processes, choosing the right tools for different situations, or turning public input into actionable policy changes. AI technologies offer powerful new possibilities—from analyzing thousands of public comments to identify key themes, to creating multilingual engagement platforms, to facilitating virtual town halls that reach broader audiences. This series goes beyond theory to teach you the practical, hands-on skills to design, implement, and measure meaningful engagement that actually works.

**Beginning September 11...** A workshop series that teaches practical skills to leverage AI and digital platforms for citizen engagement that is more inclusive, responsive, and effective at turning community voices into real policy and service improvements.

**Hosts:** This series has been curated by Professors [Danielle Allen](https://danielleallen.scholars.harvard.edu/), Harvard University, and [Beth Simone Noveck](https://thegovlab.org/beth-simone-noveck.html), Northeastern University

- Oct 10 (TBC): Defining Problems with the Public

- Oct 29: Citizen Assemblies and AI

- Nov 19: Listening to the Public with AI

- Dec 4 (TBC): Deliberating with the Public

Who should attend: Public sector professionals seeking better community engagement, elected officials and staff wanting to enhance constituent participation, civic technologists, and anyone passionate about strengthening democratic participation through responsible technology.

## Amplify: Mastering Public Communication in the AI Age

Sign up:[ innovate-us.org/amplify](https://innovate-us.org/amplify)

Government communicators face unprecedented challenges: cutting through information overload, combating disinformation, reaching diverse audiences, and maintaining public trust. AI tools offer powerful new capabilities for public communication—from drafting accessible content in multiple languages, to analyzing public sentiment on social media, to creating data visualizations that make complex policy information understandable. However, these same tools risk creating generic messaging, spreading misinformation if used carelessly, or undermining authentic human connection. Many communication professionals lack the knowledge to use AI effectively and ethically in government settings.

**Beginning October 7…** A comprehensive workshop series for communication professionals who shape public messages at all levels of government. Learn how AI tools—when used responsibly and transparently—can strengthen communication, broaden outreach, and counter disinformation while maintaining the core mission to inform, connect, and serve.

**Hosts:** Former New York Times Executive Editor and Northeastern Journalism Professor [Jill Abramson](https://burnes.northeastern.edu/people/jill-abramson/), Northeastern Professor[ John Wihbey](https://camd.northeastern.edu/people/john-p-wihbey/), and Henry Griggs serve as lead coaches, bringing deep expertise in journalism, public messaging, and strategic communication.

## About InnovateUS Workshops

All InnovateUS workshops are designed by and for public professionals, offering no-cost, practical, one-hour learning opportunities. Participants can attend individual workshops or complete a full series. A series is a multi-session workshop collection focused on a specific topic, allowing for deeper skill-building and comprehensive understanding. While you can attend as many or as few as you wish, we recommend that you participate in the whole series.

Our [extensive library of recorded AI-focused sessions ](http://innovate-us.org/workshops)is available to participants, and all attendees receive a certificate of completion.
