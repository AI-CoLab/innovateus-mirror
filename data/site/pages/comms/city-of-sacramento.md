# City of Sacramento Partners with InnovateUS to Bring Free AI Training to All City Employees

16 September 2026

*Partnership provides every city employee access to self-paced courses, live workshops, and multi-session series on responsible AI use in public service*

[InnovateUS](https://innovate-us.org/) and the City of Sacramento have announced a partnership to make free AI training available to all city employees. The partnership reflects Sacramento's commitment to ensuring every member of its workforce has the tools and knowledge to use AI responsibly, regardless of role or department.

Through the partnership, Sacramento employees now have access to a full range of InnovateUS learning formats, including self-paced courses on using generative AI in daily tasks, identifying AI opportunities in service delivery, and managing risks such as data privacy and bias; live, one-hour workshops on topics ranging from procurement and policy analysis to public communication and AI governance; and multi-session workshop series for employees who want to build deeper expertise in specific areas.

“*This partnership gives employees access to practical training that will help them understand how to use AI responsibly, securely, and effectively. By investing in our workforce, we are building the skills and confidence needed to thoughtfully adopt emerging technologies*” said Mrudul Sadanandan, Assistant IT Director, City of Sacramento.

All training is free and open to every city employee, with no cost to the City. Participants receive certificates of completion that can support their ongoing professional development.

InnovateUS is a nationally recognized learning initiative that has trained more than 200,000 public sector professionals across all 50 states. Its courses are built specifically for government work, emphasizing practical, applied skills for using AI responsibly, securely, and effectively within public institutions.

"Cities are on the front lines of public service, and that means the stakes for getting AI right are especially high," said Beth Simone Noveck, Founder of InnovateUS and Director of the Burnes Center for Social Change and the Governance Lab at Northeastern University. "Making this training free and accessible to every Sacramento employee, from frontline staff to department leaders, is exactly the kind of investment that builds public trust in how government uses these tools."

**About InnovateUS**

InnovateUS delivers free online learning for public service professionals by public service professionals on data, digital, innovation, and AI skills, with an emphasis on responsible and evidence-based approaches to technology adoption.
