# Episode 4: Pennsylvania's Pilot with ChatGPT in Government with Harrison MacRae

By Jessica Silverman · 5 August 2025

In this episode of the InnovateUS podcast, we discuss Pennsylvania's ChatGPT pilot with [⁠Harrison MacRae⁠](https://www.linkedin.com/in/harrisonmacrae/), Director of Emerging Technologies for the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. Listeners will learn about the pilot and how its findings are influencing the future adoption of AI technology in the state's government.

Check out the recording of Harrison's InnovateUS workshop [⁠here⁠](https://innovate-us.org/from-policy-to-practice-pennsylvania-s-pilot-with-chat-gpt-in-government).

Read the findings of the state's ChatGPT pilot [⁠here⁠](https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/oa/documents/programs/information-technology/documents/openai-pilot-report-2025.pdf).
