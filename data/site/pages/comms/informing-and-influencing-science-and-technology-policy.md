# Episode 2: Informing and Influencing Science and Technology Policy with Dr. Deborah Stine

By Jessica Silverman · 19 May 2025

In this episode of the InnovateUS podcast, we discuss policy analysis and the implementation of science and technology policy with [⁠Dr. Deborah Stine⁠](https://scitechpolicyacademy.com/about/) from the [⁠Science and Technology Policy Academy⁠](https://scitechpolicyacademy.com/).

Dr. Stine shares insights from her new book [⁠From Expertise to Impact: A Practical Guide to Informing and Influencing Science and Technology Policy⁠](https://scitechpolicyacademy.com/from-expertise-to-impact/)
