# 2024 in review: InnovateUS's year of impact and innovation

By Jessica Silverman · 6 January 2025

As we reflect on 2024 and enter the new year, InnovateUS is proud to share the impact we have made in advancing digital skills and innovation across the public sector. This year marked significant growth in our reach, programming, and commitment to making innovation skills and AI accessible to public service professionals worldwide.

## Our global impact

In 2024, InnovateUS reached over 50,000 learners globally with our programming, creating a diverse community of public service innovators equipped with the tools and knowledge to drive positive change in their organizations. 

State and local governments across the country also announced partnerships with InnovateUS this year to mandate our training in their jurisdictions for their workforce, including [California](https://innovation.ca.gov/blog/posts/odi-launches-calacademy-training-program/), [Maryland](https://doit.maryland.gov/SiteAssets/Pages/default/Maryland%20Department%20of%20Information%20Technology%20Provides%20Free%20AI%20Training%20to%20State%20Employees%20Thanks%20to%20Partnership%20with%20InnovateUS.pdf), [Montgomery County (MD](https://www2.montgomerycountymd.gov/mcgportalapps/Press_Detail.aspx?Item_ID=46191)), [Pennsylvania,](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/oa/newsroom/agreement-innovateus-to-train-responsible-generative-artificial-.html) and [Ohio](https://innovate-us.org/blog/state-of-ohio-partners-with-innovateus-to-launch-statewide-ai-training). 

Finally, [Google.org granted InnovateUS $5 million this year](https://blog.google/outreach-initiatives/google-org/google-org-public-sector-ai-funding/) to continue to expand our AI offerings to 100,000 public sector professionals across the country. 

### Our 2024 workshops

Our team hosted [70 free workshops](http://innovate-us.org/workshops) focused on digital transformation, innovation strategies, and artificial intelligence applications. These interactive sessions engaged more than 10,000 live participants, providing hands-on learning experiences and fostering a collaborative environment for public sector professionals to share challenges and solutions.

Our workshops covered essential topics ranging from practical applications of generative AI in government operations and ethical AI implementation to human-centered design principles and behavioral science approaches in policy-making. Sessions explored crucial areas such as data quality management, cybersecurity practices, public-private partnerships, and innovative community engagement strategies. We dedicated significant attention to emerging technologies and their impact on public service delivery, while also addressing fundamental aspects of organizational transformation through collaborative decision-making and digital accessibility.

### Our 2024 courses 

Responding to the evolving needs of public service professionals, we also introduced [four no-cost asynchronous courses](https://innovate-us.org/course) for public sector professionals.

[Human-Centered Design](https://innovate-us.org/course/human-centered-design)

This course provides participants with a comprehensive understanding of human-centered design principles and practical applications in public service contexts. Through hands-on exercises, learners develop skills to apply design thinking to real-world challenges in their organizations.

[What Works: Fast Field Scanning with AI](https://innovate-us.org/course/what-works-fast-field-scanning-with-ai)

This course empowers public professionals to leverage AI technologies in identifying and implementing evidence-based solutions to pressing public challenges. Participants learn to accelerate their research and decision-making processes while maintaining rigorous standards.

[Responsible AI for Public Professionals: Using Generative AI at Work](https://innovate-us.org/course/responsible-ai-for-public-professionals-using-generative-ai-at-work)

This course equips government workers with the knowledge and tools to effectively integrate generative AI into their daily operations. The curriculum emphasizes responsible implementation strategies that enhance public service delivery while carefully managing associated risks.

[Responsible AI for Public Professionals: Scaling AI in Your Organization](https://innovate-us.org/course/responsible-ai-for-public-professionals-scaling-ai-in-your-organization)

Designed for organizational leaders and decision-makers, this course provides a strategic framework for responsible AI adoption. Participants learn to evaluate, prioritize, and implement AI initiatives that align with their organization's goals and public service values.

### Our 2024 Google Certificate program

Finally, [in partnership with Google.org](https://blog.google/outreach-initiatives/google-org/google-org-public-sector-ai-funding/), we provided over 300 public service professionals with scholarships for [Google Certificate programs](https://innovate-us.org/google-certificates), further expanding their technical capabilities and career opportunities.

## Looking ahead 

Building on the momentum of 2024, we're excited to announce our latest offerings for the coming year.

### Expanding our course curriculum

Early in the year, we'll launch our newest course: [Responsible AI for Public Sector Legal Professionals](https://innovate-us.org/course/responsible-ai-for-public-sector-legal-professionals). This course addresses the unique intersection of artificial intelligence and legal practice in the public sector. 

### Winter workshop series: January to March 2025

Our winter workshop series will deliver a comprehensive exploration of crucial topics driving public sector transformation. These interactive sessions will cover: 

- Human-centered design

- Data & AI skills

- Innovation for social impact

- Leadership & institutional change

We're particularly excited to present real-world implementation stories from our state partners. Public service innovators from Utah and Colorado will share their firsthand experiences deploying GenAI projects, offering valuable insights and lessons learned from their journeys. ### Workshop series: AI for Literacy Achievement

Join InnovateUS for “Rethinking Reading: AI for Literacy Achievement” a workshop series hosted in partnership with the [Burnes Center for Social Change’s](https://burnes.northeastern.edu/) Equitable Literacy Initiative and [The Learning Agency](https://the-learning-agency.com/) aimed at helping education innovators explore how AI can advance K-12 literacy. 

- The series will equip learners with skills to implement AI literacy tools in an educational setting, create personalized reading intervention strategies using AI-driven insights, design inclusive literacy programs that serve diverse learning needs, and more.

- Know the perfect person to lead a workshop or share their expertise? Reach out to us at hello@innovate-us.org.
