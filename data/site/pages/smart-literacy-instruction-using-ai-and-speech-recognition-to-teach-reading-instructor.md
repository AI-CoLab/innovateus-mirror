# Smart Literacy Instruction: Using AI and Speech Recognition to Teach Reading

InnovateUS workshop · 15 April 2025 · 60 minutes

In today’s fast-evolving digital landscape, educators are constantly seeking innovative ways to support literacy development and enhance student engagement. In this hands-on workshop, participants will explore how AI-powered applications and speech recognition software can personalize reading instruction, providing real-time feedback on pronunciation, fluency, and comprehension. They will also learn how these technologies can help students with diverse learning needs, such as English language learners, by offering tailored learning experiences and fostering independent reading practices.

## Learning goals

- Explore how AI-powered applications and speech recognition software can personalize reading instruction, providing real-time feedback on pronunciation, fluency, and comprehension

- Learn how AI technologies can help students with diverse learning needs
