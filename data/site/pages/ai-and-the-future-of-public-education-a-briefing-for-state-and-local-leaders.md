# AI and the Future of Public Education – A Briefing for State and Local Leaders

InnovateUS workshop · 20 May 2025 · 60 minutes

Explore how AI is influencing literacy and learning at scale. This session shares perspectives on how educational leaders can navigate implementation at the district and state levels. It will highlight strategic opportunities, governance considerations, and examples of how public education systems are responding to the evolving role of AI.

## Learning goals

- Identify key strategies for implementing AI literacy tools at district and state levels, including governance frameworks and policy considerations
- Analyze real-world examples of how public education systems are successfully integrating AI into curriculum and administrative processes
- Develop an actionable roadmap for educational leaders to evaluate, pilot, and scale AI learning technologies while addressing equity and access concerns
