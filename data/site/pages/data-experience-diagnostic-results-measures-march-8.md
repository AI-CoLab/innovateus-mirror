# Data Experience Diagnostic: Results > Measures

InnovateUS workshop · 8 March 2022

Join us to hear from Marc Hébert, UX/Service Design Manager of the San Francisco Human Services Agency, and learn how to diagnose your team's organizational data practices, prioritize what bite-sized changes to make first, and then develop practical next steps to integrate into your current process on any data-related project.

## Learning goals

- Diagnose your team's organizational data practices
- Prioritize what bite-sized changes to make first
- Develop practical next steps to integrate into your current process on any data-related project

## Speakers

- **Marc Hébert**, UX/Service Design Manager, San Francisco Human Services Agency
