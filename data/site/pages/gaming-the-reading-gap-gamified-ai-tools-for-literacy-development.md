# Gaming the Reading Gap: Gamified AI Tools for Literacy Development

InnovateUS workshop · 11 February 2025 · 60 minutes

Dive into the world of gamified AI tools that are transforming how we assess and support reading skill development. Gamification is emerging as a powerful way to engage students while providing personalized, real-time feedback on their literacy progress. Participants will explore cutting-edge AI-powered platforms that use game mechanics to motivate students, track their growth, and target specific areas for improvement. These tools can make reading practice more interactive, accessible, and effective - especially for students who need it most.
