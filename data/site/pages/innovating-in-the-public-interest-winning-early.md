# Innovating in the Public Interest: Winning Early

InnovateUS workshop · 9 April 2025 · 60 minutes

Great projects begin with early wins. We will talk about the importance of driving for early wins – and of using them to build understanding of how collaborators are each evaluated and rewarded for successes.  Early successes can become the foundation for longer-term initiatives.

## Learning goals

- **Design Early Wins** – Learn how to structure quick, targeted successes that build trust, demonstrate impact, and reinforce public-sector credibility.

- **Navigate Cross-Sector Dynamics** – Understand how different organizations measure success and leverage early achievements to strengthen long-term collaboration.

- **Overcome Skepticism** – Explore strategies to gain buy-in from internal and external stakeholders by demonstrating clear public value.
