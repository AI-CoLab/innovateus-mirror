# From Findings to Fixes: Improving Accessibility in Public Digital Services

InnovateUS workshop · 21 January 2027 · 60 minutes · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

AI-assisted tools increasingly offer suggested fixes or automated remediation for inaccessible websites and documents. These tools may work well for straightforward content, but their performance can vary considerably with forms, STEM materials, unconventional tables, and other complex content.

This workshop examines how public-sector teams can use AI-assisted remediation while distinguishing between suggested changes and verified accessibility. Participants will consider how the complexity, public importance, and expected use of content may affect the appropriate level of review. The session will also examine how agencies can validate automated fixes and decide when specialized remediation software, accessibility professionals, or testing by assistive-technology users may be appropriate.

Part of the series [Using AI for Digital Accessibility](https://innovate-us.org/workshop-series/using-ai-for-digital-accessibility).
