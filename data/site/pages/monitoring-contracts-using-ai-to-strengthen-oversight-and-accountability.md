# Monitoring Contracts: Using AI to Strengthen Oversight and Accountability

InnovateUS workshop

This session examines how AI can support contract management after awards are made. Participants will explore how AI-assisted tools can help monitor performance, identify compliance issues, and support ongoing oversight—while reinforcing accountability, documentation, and public trust.
