# Defining Your Problem

InnovateUS workshop · 2 February 2023 · 90 minutes

Join us to hear from Beth Noveck, chief innovation officer for the State of New Jersey, where participants will learn how to narrow down tough problems through creating a hypothesis and defining the root causes. Participants will walk away with tools for clearly articulating and reframing the problems and root causes of projects that they are working on.

## Learning goals

- Learn how to narrow down tough problems through creating a hypothesis and defining the root causes
- Understand the tools for clearly articulating and reframing the problems and root causes of projects that they are working on
