# Powerful Partnerships

InnovateUS workshop · 11 August 2022

Join us to hear from Neil Kleiman, Senior Fellow with The Burnes Center for Social Change, about how collaboration between private, public, and non-profit sectors have the potential to accomplish far more than any organization from a single sector can do. In this one-hour virtual workshop, we will discuss different examples of partnerships, the advantages of cross-sector partnerships, and how to manage implementation challenges and risks.

## Learning goals

- Discuss different examples of partnerships
- Learn the advantages of cross-sector partnerships
- Manage implementation challenges and risks
