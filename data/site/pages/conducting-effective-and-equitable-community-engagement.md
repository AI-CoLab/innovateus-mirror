# Conducting Effective and Equitable Community Engagement

InnovateUS workshop · 7 November 2024 · 90 minutes

Join Sofía Bosch Gómez, Postdoctoral Fellow at The Burnes Center for Social Change, for a dynamic 90-minute virtual workshop designed to empower participants with the skills to harness the collective wisdom of communities. This session will introduce innovative tools and processes that help participants collaboratively define problems and generate creative solutions. Through interactive training, you’ll explore cutting-edge engagement strategies and gain insights into fostering collaboration in ways that are both effective and equitable.

## Learning goals

- Learn how to apply design methods to engage communities in an equitable manner.

- Develop a comprehensive protocol for meaningful and effective community engagement.

- Discover the value of participation and engagement across various international contexts, and understand how to tailor your approach accordingly.
