# From Individual Prompts to Repeatable Workflows

InnovateUS workshop · 5 March 2027 · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

Move beyond isolated tasks and learn how to integrate AI into recurring work processes. Through guided exercises, practice breaking down a workflow into manageable steps, connecting AI-assisted activities, and identifying where human review and decision-making are essential.

Practical outcome: Design an AI-supported workflow for a recurring task, with clearly defined steps, responsibilities, and safeguards.

Part of the series [The Prompting Lab: Integrating AI into Your Public Sector Work](https://innovate-us.org/workshop-series/the-prompting-lab-series).
