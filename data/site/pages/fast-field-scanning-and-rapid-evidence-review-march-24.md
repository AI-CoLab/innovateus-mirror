# Fast Field Scanning and Rapid Evidence Review

InnovateUS workshop · 24 March 2022

Join us to hear from Peter Bragge, Senior Research Fellow at BehaviourWorks Australia, Monash Sustainability Institute, about how to find solutions that have been tried before, how to assess the success of those solutions, and better evaluate whether solutions that worked elsewhere may help you in your work.

## Learning goals

- Learn how to find solutions that have been tried before
- Learn to assess the success of those solutions
- Understand how to better evaluate whether solutions that worked elsewhere may help you in your work
