# Equity, Access, and Public Trust: Designing Inclusive AI Literacy Policies

InnovateUS workshop

This workshop focuses on how policy decisions can either reduce or reinforce disparities in educational outcomes. Participants will explore strategies for ensuring equitable access, protecting student data, engaging communities, and building public trust in AI-supported educational initiatives.
