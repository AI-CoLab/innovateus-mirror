# Using Behavioral Insights for the Public Sector

InnovateUS workshop · 13 April 2023 · 90 minutes

Join Leah Everist, a Senior Advisor for the Behavioral Insights Team, and Marta Garnelo Caamano,  Principal Advisor at BIT Americas, for this 90-minute virtual training where participants will learn about behavioral science concepts and explore ways that these behavioral insights can help them develop and test policies and services that encourage individuals to make better decisions. Participants will walk away with a better understanding of how governments at the state and local level have used behavioral insights and how they might incorporate them into their work.
