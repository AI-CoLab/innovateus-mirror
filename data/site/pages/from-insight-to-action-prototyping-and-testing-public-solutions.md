# From Insight to Action: Prototyping and Testing Public Solutions

InnovateUS workshop

This workshop focuses on turning insights into action through rapid prototyping and testing. Participants will learn how to develop low-risk prototypes, gather meaningful feedback, and iterate solutions in ways that improve services while working within public sector constraints.

Part of the series [Human-Centered Design](https://innovate-us.org/workshop-series/human-centered-design).
