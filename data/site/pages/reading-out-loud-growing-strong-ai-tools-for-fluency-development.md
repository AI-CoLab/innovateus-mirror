# Reading Out Loud, Growing Strong: AI Tools for Fluency Development

InnovateUS workshop · 12 March 2025 · 60 minutes

Focus on mastering reading fluence using AI-powered tools that leverage Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) to guide read-aloud practice and provide personalized feedback. Fluency is a critical component of literacy, and ASR technology is enabling students to practice reading aloud with immediate, tailored guidance. Through hands-on experience, participants will explore ASR-driven tools for literacy instruction to support diverse learners, improve reading fluency, and foster a love of reading.
