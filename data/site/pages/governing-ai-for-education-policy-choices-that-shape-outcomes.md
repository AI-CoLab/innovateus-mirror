# Governing AI for Education: Policy Choices That Shape Outcomes

InnovateUS workshop

This session examines the policy decisions that determine how AI is introduced into educational initiatives, including standards setting, accountability, data use, and oversight. Participants will explore how governance choices influence instructional coherence, equity, and long-term sustainability.
