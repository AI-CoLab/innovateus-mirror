# Reimagining Public Institutions: Rethinking Leadership for Organizational Transformation

InnovateUS workshop · 18 March 2025 · 90 minutes

Join Christian Bason, Ph.D., Co-founder of Transition Collective, for a 90-minute virtual training on the evolving role of leadership in driving institutional change in the public sector. As governments face increasing complexity, leaders must move beyond traditional management models to foster adaptability, collaboration, and innovation.

This session will explore how public sector leaders can create conditions for meaningful change by shifting mindsets, rethinking structures, and enabling teams to navigate uncertainty. Drawing from real-world examples, Christian will offer insights into new leadership practices that empower organizations to be more responsive, human-centered, and impactful.

## Learning goals

- Lead institutional change with a future-oriented mindset
- Balance structure with flexibility in decision-making
- Foster a culture of trust, learning, and collaboration
- Navigate challenges in transforming bureaucratic systems

- **Christian Bason**, Co-founder, Transition Collective and former Chief Executive, Danish Design Center
