# An Introduction to AI for the Public Sector

InnovateUS workshop · 6 October 2023 · 90 minutes

Join us for a 90-minute virtual workshop with Marci Harris, CEO and Co-Founder of POPVOX, a nonpartisan platform for civic engagement and Executive Director of the POPVOX Foundation. This workshop is geared towards public sector professionals who are new to artificial intelligence (AI) and are looking for an "AI Explainer" that covers the differences between machine learning, natural language processing, and generative AI, and highlights their uses for governing. Participants will be introduced to basic use cases for AI in government, and will learn a tiered approach to incorporating AI in their everyday work, starting with low-risk task-related uses that anyone can start experimenting with today.

## Learning goals

- Become familiar with differences between machine learning, natural language processing, and generative AI , and understand their uses for governing
- Learn about the basic use cases for AI in government
- Learn a tiered approach to incorporating AI in their everyday work
