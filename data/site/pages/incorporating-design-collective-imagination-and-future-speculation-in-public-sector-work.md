# Incorporating Design, Collective Imagination, and Future Speculation in Public Sector Work

InnovateUS workshop · 25 June 2024 · 60 minutes

Join us for this 60-minute virtual workshop where Oskar Stokholm Østergaard, Design & Futures Lead at the Danish Design Center will talk about the importance of thinking about alternative futures in public sector work.

## Learning goals

- Learn about ways to launch design-focused missions, engage the collective imagination in thinking about possible futures, and how to build experiments and cultures that are vision-driven
- Understand ideas on how to expand what we imagine to be possible, and how to move from ideas to concrete experiments
