# Building Support and Leading Your Team Through Change

InnovateUS workshop · 9 February 2027 · 60 minutes · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

Leading change requires building support in every direction: with the teams putting improvements into practice, the senior leaders who can help move them forward, and the colleagues whose work may be affected. Learn practical strategies for involving your team early, communicating why a change matters, and making a compelling case to decision-makers. Through real government examples, explore how to understand different perspectives, address concerns constructively, and build the trust, relationships, and shared commitment needed to turn plans into action.

## Learning goals

- Identify ways to involve their team early in shaping a change, so the people putting it into practice help design it.

- Explain why a change matters in terms that resonate with senior leaders, and outline a brief case for their support.

- Anticipate concerns from colleagues whose work may be affected, and plan a constructive way to address them.

Part of the series [From Ideas to Action: Leading and Implementing Change as a Public Sector Manager](https://innovate-us.org/workshop-series/from-ideas-to-action-leading-and-implementing-change-in-government).
