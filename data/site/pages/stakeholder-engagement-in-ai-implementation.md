# Stakeholder Engagement in AI Implementation

InnovateUS workshop · 6 October 2025 · 60 minutes

Successful AI implementation in law enforcement requires active involvement from a broad set of stakeholders, including internal departments, oversight bodies, and community representatives. This session will help executives develop stakeholder engagement strategies and processes that support trust, transparency, and shared ownership throughout the AI lifecycle—from problem definition to deployment.

## Learning goals

- Develop stakeholder engagement strategies and processes that support trust, transparency, and shared ownership throughout the AI lifecycle
- Understand the stakeholder engagement process from problem definition to deployment
