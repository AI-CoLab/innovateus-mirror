# Building and Buying Legal AI

InnovateUS workshop · 28 October 2026 · 60 minutes

The market for legal AI tools is expanding rapidly. At the same time, many organizations are creating their own custom assistants, knowledge systems, and workflow tools. This session examines how public-sector legal offices can evaluate, procure, configure, and govern AI systems. Participants will learn what questions to ask vendors, when custom solutions may make sense, and how to design safeguards that support responsible adoption.
