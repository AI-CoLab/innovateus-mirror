# Designing with New Tools: Using AI to Support Human-Centered Service Design

InnovateUS workshop

This session explores how AI tools can support human-centered design work, from synthesizing research findings to generating design options and improving accessibility. Participants will examine practical examples of AI-assisted service design and discuss how to use these tools responsibly while keeping people, equity, and accountability at the center.

Part of the series [Human-Centered Design](https://innovate-us.org/workshop-series/human-centered-design).
