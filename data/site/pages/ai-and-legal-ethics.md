# AI and Legal Ethics

InnovateUS workshop · 13 May 2026 · 60 minutes

As artificial intelligence rapidly transforms the legal landscape, practitioners face a pivotal moment in redefining how law is researched, practiced, and delivered. This session examines the latest ethics opinions and judicial decisions shaping the use of AI in legal practice, alongside the emerging technologies poised to further reshape the profession. Attendees will explore the evolving opportunities AI presents for efficiency and innovation, while grappling with the ethical responsibilities lawyers must navigate as AI becomes an increasingly integral part of how they serve their clients.

## Learning goals

1. Identify and apply relevant ethics opinions and court rulings governing AI use to real-world scenarios encountered in legal practice.

 2. Evaluate AI tools for practical utility and compliance risk by assessing their fit within legal workflows and professional responsibility obligations.

 3. Build a framework for responsible AI adoption that anticipates regulatory shifts and upholds client obligations.
