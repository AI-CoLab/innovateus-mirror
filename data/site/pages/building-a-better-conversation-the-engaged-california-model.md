# Building a Better Conversation: The Engaged California Model

InnovateUS workshop · 20 November 2025 · 60 minutes

Discover how California is using digital tools and AI to help residents discuss complex issues, from wildfire recovery to public priorities, and turn community insights into real policy action. **Moderated by Beth Simone Noveck. **

## Learning goals

- Learn how California is using AI to help residents discuss complex issues
- Discover how to turn community insights into real policy action

## Speakers

- **Micah Weinberg**, Nonresident Scholar, Carnegie Endowment for International Peace
- **Jeffery Marino**, Director, Office of Data and Innovation, State of California
- **Beth Simone Noveck**, Founder of InnovateUS and Director, Burnes Center for Change and the GovLab

Part of the series [Reboot Democracy: Designing Democratic Engagement for the AI Era](https://innovate-us.org/workshop-series/democratic-engagement).
