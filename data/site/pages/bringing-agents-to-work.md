# Bringing Agents to Work

InnovateUS workshop · 7 December 2026 · 60 minutes · upcoming: registration on the workshop page

Technology adoption is ultimately a people challenge. How do agencies help staff move from occasional AI use to meaningful integration into everyday work? This session examines strategies for workforce adoption, training, change management, experimentation, and learning. Participants will explore how organizations are helping employees identify high-value use cases, redesign workflows, build confidence in agentic tools, and create feedback loops that improve both performance and governance over time.
