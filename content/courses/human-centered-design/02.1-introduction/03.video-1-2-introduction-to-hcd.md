# Video 1.2: Introduction to HCD

Human Centered Design (HCD) is presented as both a mindset and a method, emphasizing the importance of having both a philosophical approach and specific process skills. The five phases of HCD—empathy, define, ideate, prototype, and test—are briefly outlined, highlighting the iterative and messy nature of the process. The Double Diamond model is introduced as a conceptualization of the HCD process, distinguishing between divergent and convergent thinking, and the importance of involving users throughout the entire design process is emphasized for effective implementation of design thinking.
