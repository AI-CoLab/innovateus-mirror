# 1\. Introduction

### Introduction to Human Centered Design

Welcome to the Human Centered Design course. This first module introduces the concept of human-centered design - a method that prioritizes starting with people to better understand their needs and motivations, ultimately leading to innovation when combined with considerations of viability and feasibility. After this module, you will be able to demonstrate an understanding of the human-centered design process, including processes and design tools for each step.
