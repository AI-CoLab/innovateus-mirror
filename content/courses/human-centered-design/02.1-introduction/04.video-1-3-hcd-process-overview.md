# Video 1.3: HCD Process Overview

This module focuses on developing empathy with customers in the context of human centered design (HCD). It emphasizes the importance of understanding customers' needs and challenges by stepping into their shoes, free from assumptions and biases. The course encourages participants to define a problem for HCD, map stakeholders, create empathy interview questions, and generate a journey map, with a specific focus on increasing engagement and belonging in the workplace.
