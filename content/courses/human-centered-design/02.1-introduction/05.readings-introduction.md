# Readings: Introduction

[Universal Methods of Design](https://www.amazon.com/Universal-Methods-Design-Expanded-Revised/dp/1631597485/ref=sr_1_3?crid=2K6MU9HYRWY2X\&keywords=the+pocket+universal+methods+of+design\&qid=1706641691\&s=books\&sprefix=Universal+Methods+of+Design%2Cstripbooks%2C234\&sr=1-3)

Bella Martin + Bruce Hanington

125 Methods and approaches, organized and cataloged so you can understand methods, get references for further reading, and see case studies. It’s a great reference.

[The Field Guide to Human Centered Design](https://www.designkit.org/).

The classic IDEO guide to HCD. Lots of case studies, examples, as well as How To. The Guide can be downloaded or used online.

[Observatory of Public Sector Innovation](https://oecd-opsi.org/)

The OECD’s innovation in government website. It says, in About Us: “Turn the new into the normal. We equip governments and public servants with fresh insights, knowledge, tools, and connections to help them explore new possibilities.” There are lots of frameworks and case studies from around the world.

[Transform with Design](https://utorontopress.com/9781487533298/transform-with-design/)

edited by Jochen Schwietzer, Sihem Benmahmoud-Jouni, Sebastian Fixon

Stories of how people implemented HCD in various organizations, from large corporations to health care institutions and government. (Shameless promotion: This book has a chapter about Virginia Hamilton).

[Hack Your Bureaucracy: Get Things Done No Matter What Your Role on Any Team](https://www.amazon.com/Hack-Your-Bureaucracy-Things-Matter/dp/0306827751)

Marina Nitse and Nick Sinai

Not a book about HCD per se, but an important book about getting things done in a government setting, focusing on innovation and creating a modern government.

[Recoding America: Why Government Is Failing in the Digital Age and How We Can Do Better](https://www.amazon.com/Recoding-America-Government-Failing-Digital/dp/B0C5S25MJC/ref=sr_1_1?hvadid=647670935245\&hvdev=c\&hvlocphy=1013585\&hvnetw=g\&hvqmt=e\&hvrand=8896856107269667376\&hvtargid=kwd-1956360866540\&hydadcr=19052_13375783\&keywords=recording+america\&qid=1706642664\&sr=8-1)

## [Recoding America: Why Government Is Failing in the Digital Age and How We Can Do Better](https://www.amazon.com/Recoding-America-Government-Failing-Digital/dp/B0C5S25MJC/ref=sr_1_1?hvadid=647670935245\&hvdev=c\&hvlocphy=1013585\&hvnetw=g\&hvqmt=e\&hvrand=8896856107269667376\&hvtargid=kwd-1956360866540\&hydadcr=19052_13375783\&keywords=recording+america\&qid=1706642664\&sr=8-1)

Jennifer Pahlka

A description of how government has fallen behind, the issues and problems inherent in government agencies, and examples of how innovation can happen within the constraints of bureaucracies.
