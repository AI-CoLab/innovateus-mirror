# Readings: Ideate

Brainstorming

[IDEO brainstorming rules](https://www.ideou.com/blogs/inspiration/7-simple-rules-of-brainstorming)

A list of rules for brainstorming and a poster you can download to place on the wall.

[Protobot](https://protobot.org/#en) Molly Wilson

An amazing website that helps loosen you up for ideation. Press the Randomise button, and you will have a new invention to figure out. It's great to use before going into a brainstorming session.

Prioritizing Solutions

[Prioritization Matrices](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/prioritization-matrices/) Neilson Norman Group

This is a good article that describes various ways of prioritizing ideas and which should be moved forward into prototyping.
