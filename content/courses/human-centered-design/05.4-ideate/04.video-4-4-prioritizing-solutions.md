# Video 4.4: Prioritizing Solutions

Prioritizing Solutions

[Next: Readings](https://course.innovate-us.org/mod/page/view.php?id=393)

In this video, the focus is on selecting the best idea for a project after the ideation phase. Prioritization methods include assessing the scale of the problem, using voting dots, and evaluating impact versus effort. The video introduces a concept template for developing chosen ideas and emphasizes the importance of refining ideas and identifying key questions for prototyping and testing.
