Brainstorming Methods

[Next: Video 4.4](https://course.innovate-us.org/mod/h5pactivity/view.php?id=392)

This video explores two methods for brainstorming ideas: Crazy Eights and the Creative Matrix. Crazy Eights involves sketching eight different ideas on a sheet of paper to encourage thinking beyond initial concepts. The Creative Matrix is a tool where 'how might we' questions are listed at the top, and participants brainstorm solutions within relevant categories, fostering rapid idea generation.
