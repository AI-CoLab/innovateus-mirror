# 4\. Ideate

### Ideate

In this module, you will apply methods from the ideate process step to generate and evaluate ideas and develop a concept solution. you will be able to generate different ideas to solve a problem and evaluate a set of ideas based on their customer desirability, technical feasibility, and viability. Additionally, you will be able to develop a solution concept that explains the customer, the problem addressed, how it works, assumptions made, and the next steps for implementation.
