# Video 4.2: Brainstorming Overview

[Previous: Video 4.1](https://course.innovate-us.org/mod/h5pactivity/view.php?id=389)

Brainstorming Overview

[Next: Video 4.3](https://course.innovate-us.org/mod/h5pactivity/view.php?id=391)

This video on ideation discusses the concept of 'how might we' questions as a valuable tool for generating innovative ideas. Emphasizing the importance of balancing questions, the video encourages avoiding those that are too narrow or too broad. The video also introduces structured methods for brainstorming, highlighting rules for effective brainstorming, and recommends warming up with creative exercises to encourage lateral thinking.
