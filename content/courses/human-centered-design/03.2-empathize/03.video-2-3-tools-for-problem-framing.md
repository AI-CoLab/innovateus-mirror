# Video 2.3: Tools for Problem Framing

In this segment, two tools, the 5 Whys and a Checklist, are introduced for crafting an initial problem statement in human centered design. The 5 Whys tool is explained as a method to identify the root cause of a problem by iteratively asking "why" five times, leading to a refined problem statement. A checklist is provided to ensure that the problem statement meets criteria such as being a human, subjective challenge focused on discovery and learning, solvable with a product or service, devoid of embedded solutions, and not assuming the customer's needs.
