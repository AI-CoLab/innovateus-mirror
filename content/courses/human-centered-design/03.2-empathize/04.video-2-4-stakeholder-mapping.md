# Video 2.4: Stakeholder Mapping

Human centered design is highlighted as a problem-solving approach that involves designing solutions in collaboration with those directly affected by the problem and relevant stakeholders, including customer-facing staff in government. The importance of keeping customers and stakeholders engaged throughout the process is emphasized to ensure the resulting solutions genuinely meet their needs. The video introduces stakeholder mapping as a key activity to identify and understand the individuals and organizations that should be involved in the project, helping to inform plans for research and engagement throughout the human centered design process.
