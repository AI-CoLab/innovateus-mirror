# 2\. Empathize

### Empathize

In this module, you will apply methods from the empathize process step to develop a problem statement, stakeholder map, empathy map, and journey map. After this module, you will be able to collect information from an interview to compose an empathy map, define a human-centered problem that is solution-agnostic, map stakeholders, identify customer groups for research and influential groups for relationship building, and create a journey map that illustrates a customer experience.
