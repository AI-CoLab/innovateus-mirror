# Video 2.2: Problem Framing

This module emphasizes the importance of problem framing in human centered design, challenging the conventional approach of immediately jumping to solutions. It suggests that problem framing is at the heart of innovation and encourages considering diverse perspectives to define problems accurately. The module concludes by emphasizing that human centered design is suitable for addressing issues related to human behavior that require continuous learning and testing, in contrast to technical challenges with straightforward solutions.
