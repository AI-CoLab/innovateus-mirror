Empathy Interviews

[Next: Video 2.6](https://course.innovate-us.org/mod/h5pactivity/view.php?id=379)

This video focuses on the importance and best practices of conducting empathy interviews in human centered design. Empathy interviews, rooted in ethnographic practices, are described as 1:1 discussions with a conversational tone aimed at understanding customers' beliefs, motivations, and behaviors. The facilitators are encouraged to define interview goals, clarify roles, establish rapport, focus on gathering stories, ask open-ended questions, and maintain a curious mindset to better understand customers' unbiased needs. The recap underscores the significance of empathy interviews as a primary tool for customer research, emphasizing the need for open-ended questions, active listening, and curiosity.
