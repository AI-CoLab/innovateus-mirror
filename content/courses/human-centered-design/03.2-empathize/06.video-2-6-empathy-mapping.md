# Video 2.6: Empathy Mapping

This video introduces the Empathy Map as a visualization tool for documenting the customer experience during empathy interviews in human centered design. The map focuses on capturing what the interviewees are doing, feeling, saying, and thinking, providing a structured way to understand their experiences. The video emphasizes that Empathy Maps help identify inconsistencies or themes in the data, allowing designers to generate insights, understand motivations, pain points, and needs of the customers.
