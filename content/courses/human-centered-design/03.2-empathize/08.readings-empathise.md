**Problem Framing**

**\\ **

**[What’s Your Problem?](https://www.amazon.com/Whats-Your-Problem-Toughest-Problems/dp/1633697223/ref=asc_df_1633697223/?tag=hyprod-20\&linkCode=df0\&hvadid=459617407428\&hvpos=\&hvnetw=g\&hvrand=8754092248425995210\&hvpone=\&hvptwo=\&hvqmt=\&hvdev=c\&hvdvcmdl=\&hvlocint=\&hvlocphy=9032077\&hvtargid=pla-893846971423\&psc=1\&mcid=3a37794037ee3778b02df12182709e39\&gclid=Cj0KCQiA2eKtBhDcARIsAEGTG41LXTC-etAZPyb8UAvn11yWdY21zlOgvTl40LiA7FHkjuGmshKkgB4aAuj9EALw_wcB) Thomas Wedell-Wedellsborg**

**This terrific book introduces a method for framing and reframing problems. It has many examples, activities, and visual prompts to help you ensure you are anchoring on the right problem.**

**\\ **

**[Abstraction Ladder](https://www.luma-institute.com/abstraction-laddering/)**

**Luma Institute’s description of how to use the Abstraction Ladder when looking at a problem, to use various lenses before landing on the right problem.**

**\\ **

**[The 5 Whys](https://www.mindtools.com/a3mi00v/5-whys)**

**A good article about the origin and ways to use the 5 Whys process.**

**\\ **

**Stakeholder Mapping**

**\\ **

**[Stakeholder Mapping](https://www.interaction-design.org/literature/article/map-the-stakeholders)**

**The Interaction Design Foundation’s article and video about how to create and use a stakeholder map.**

**\\ **

**[Stakeholder Map](https://civicmakers.com/resource/stakeholder-identification-template/)**

**Civic Makers’ tool for stakeholder mapping, both available as a PDF and as a template in Mural**

**\\ **

**[Asset Mapping](https://healthpolicy.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/2023-08/tw_cba20.pdf) UCLA Center for Health Policy Research**

**This is an older document (it mentions Mapquest), but very good at the hows and whys of asset mapping in a community. This is within the healthcare context and is very relevant to government.**

**\\ **

**Empathy Interviews**

**\\ **

**[Empathy FieldGuide](https://hci.stanford.edu/courses/cs147/2022/wi/readings/FIELDGUIDE-Screen-DTBC-March-2015-V2.pdf) Stanford d. school**

**A practical and useful guide to help you plan customer research, from empathy interviews to observation.**

**\\ **

**[Conduct Empathy Interviews](https://practices.learningaccelerator.org/strategies/activity-conduct-empathy-interviews-with-stakeholders) The Learning Accelerator**

**A well-designed guide to conducting empathy interviews, with other links and sources to explore.**

**\\ **

**Journey Mapping**

**\\ **

**[Draw Toast](https://www.ted.com/talks/tom_wujec_got_a_wicked_problem_first_tell_me_how_you_make_toast?language=en) Tom Wujak**

**It's a great video to kick off a journey mapping activity. Wujak uses the process of drawing toast to describe the process of mapping a process.**
