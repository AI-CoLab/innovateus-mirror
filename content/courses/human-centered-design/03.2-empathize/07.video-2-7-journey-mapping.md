# Video 2.7: Journey Mapping

[Previous: Video 2.6](https://course.innovate-us.org/mod/h5pactivity/view.php?id=379)

Journey Mapping

[Next:Readings](https://course.innovate-us.org/mod/page/view.php?id=381)

This video introduces journey mapping as a tool in human centered design, illustrating how it helps understand and improve customer experiences with products or services. Examples demonstrate how journey maps can identify gaps, pain points, and improvement opportunities in various customer interactions. The video emphasizes that journey maps can be created using different methods, with a focus on the specific problem being addressed, and provides guidance on selecting a customer or persona, outlining the journey, and choosing relevant data for mapping.
