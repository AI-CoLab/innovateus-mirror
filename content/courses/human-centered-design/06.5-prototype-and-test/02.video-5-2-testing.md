# Video 5.2: Testing

[Previous: Video 5.1](https://course.innovate-us.org/mod/h5pactivity/view.php?id=395)

Testing

[Next: Readings](https://course.innovate-us.org/mod/page/view.php?id=397)

The video introduces a template to help plan testing for prototypes, emphasizing the importance of careful consideration regarding the prototype's purpose, target audience, test duration, and success criteria. Examples illustrate the concept of minimally viable products and the efficient gathering of feedback. The video concludes by highlighting the iterative nature of prototyping, emphasizing the PACE acronym for deciding how to iterate based on feedback and the use of learning cards to analyze observations and plan the next steps in the design process.
