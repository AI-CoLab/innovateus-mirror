# 5\. Prototype and Test

### Prototype and Test

In this module, you will demonstrate an understanding of rapid prototyping and this knowledge to prototype and test their concept with a customer. you will be able to describe the value and characteristics of rapid prototyping, create a prototyping plan for their concept, create a low-fidelity prototype of their solution, and test their low-fidelity prototype with a customer to gather feedback.
