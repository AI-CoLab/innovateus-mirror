# Video 3.1: Define

In this module, the focus is on refining a problem statement using information gathered from customer research. The importance of avoiding biased assumptions and seeing the customer's perspective is highlighted through an anecdote about a non-profit realizing their preconceived solutions skewed their understanding of customer needs.
