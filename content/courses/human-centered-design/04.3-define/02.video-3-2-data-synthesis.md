# Video 3.2: Data Synthesis

[Previous: Video 3.1](https://course.innovate-us.org/mod/h5pactivity/view.php?id=383)

Data Synthesis

[Next: Video 3.3](https://course.innovate-us.org/mod/h5pactivity/view.php?id=385)

In this video, the focus is on turning collected data, including journey maps and empathy interviews, into actionable insights for problem-solving. The discussion emphasizes that human centered design deals with unstructured, messy data such as behaviors, preferences, and emotions. The process involves organizing data into themes using Affinity Maps, which helps identify common patterns and surprises, leading to insights that are crucial for generating "How Might We" questions and potential solutions.
