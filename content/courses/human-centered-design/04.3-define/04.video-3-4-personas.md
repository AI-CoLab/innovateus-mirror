# Video 3.4: Personas

In this video, the focus is on personas, which are fictional characters derived from customer research, capturing key characteristics, motivations, and goals. Personas are used to maintain focus on solving customer needs, particularly when dealing with complex issues such as policy challenges. The video concludes by cautioning against using personas as a substitute for direct customer research and emphasizes the need to periodically update personas to reflect changes in customers' worlds.
