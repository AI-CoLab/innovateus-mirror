# Video 3.3: How Might We Questions

[Previous: Video 3.2](https://course.innovate-us.org/mod/h5pactivity/view.php?id=384)

How Might We Questions

[Next: Video 3.4](https://course.innovate-us.org/mod/h5pactivity/view.php?id=386)

In this video, the focus is on the significance and crafting of "How Might We" (HMW) questions in the human centered design process. These questions are open-ended, generative queries that aid in reframing problems and generating a diverse range of ideas. The video provides guidelines for crafting effective HMW questions, such as avoiding embedded solutions, centering on research findings, emphasizing desired outcomes, using the Goldilocks test for balance, and framing questions positively. Additionally, it introduces various frameworks, including creating analogies, focusing on emotions, amping up the good, exploring the opposite, and questioning assumptions, with examples related to workplace issues. The overall goal is to foster creativity, challenge assumptions, and open up new possibilities in problem-solving.
