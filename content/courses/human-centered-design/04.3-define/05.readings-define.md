**Data Synthesis**

**[How to Write Compelling User Research Insights in 6 Steps](https://dscout.com/people-nerds/writing-user-insights) D Scout**

**This is an easy-to-read (and follow) guide to moving from customer research to insights.**

**\
\\ **

**[How Exactly to you find User Insights?](https://uxdesign.cc/how-exactly-do-you-find-insights-from-qualitative-user-research-603bcafbc8b3) UX Collective**

**This is a well-written article about how to synthesize the data you have collected in research into insights to be used for ideation.**

**\\ **

**HMW questions**

**\\ **

**[Using “How Might We” Questions to Ideate on the Right Problems](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-might-we-questions/) Neilson Norman Group**

**This is an excellent description of ways to develop good How Might We statements that are generative and scaled at the right level.**

**\\ **

**[How Might We](https://designsprintkit.withgoogle.com/methodology/phase1-understand/hmw-sharing-and-affinity-mapping) Google Design Sprint**

**In addition to providing advice about good How Might We questions, this is part of a larger resource for conducting Design Sprints.**
