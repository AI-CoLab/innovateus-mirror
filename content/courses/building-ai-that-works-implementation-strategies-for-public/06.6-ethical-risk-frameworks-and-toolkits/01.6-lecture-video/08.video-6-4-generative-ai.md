# Video 6.4: Generative AI

This video discusses the unique risks posed by Generative AI (GenAI) and the additional guidance provided by NIST to address these challenges. It emphasizes the importance of structured public feedback mechanisms, such as field testing, AI red-teaming, and participatory engagement methods, in GenAI risk management. The video also stresses the importance of demographically and interdisciplinarily diverse participants in these feedback methods to identify flaws in the varying contexts where GenAI will be used.
