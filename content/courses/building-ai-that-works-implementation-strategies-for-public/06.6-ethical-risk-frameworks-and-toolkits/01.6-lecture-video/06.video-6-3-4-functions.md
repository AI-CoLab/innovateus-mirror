# Video 6.3: 4 functions

This video discusses the four functions (Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage) outlined in the AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) that can help reduce risk and maximize benefits in each of the seven characteristics of trustworthy AI. It explains how the Govern function focuses on establishing the ecosystem for the other three functions to succeed, while Map, Measure, and Manage are iterative steps for managing risk throughout the AI lifecycle. The video also emphasizes the importance of tailoring the AI RMF activities to an organization's specific context and treating the framework as a tool for thinking and judicious action rather than a checklist.
