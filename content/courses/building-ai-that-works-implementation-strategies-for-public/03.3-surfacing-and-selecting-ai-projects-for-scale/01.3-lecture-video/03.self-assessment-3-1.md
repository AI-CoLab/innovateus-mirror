What is the biggest key to successful AI projects in government?

- [x] Being problem-led and focusing on daily challenges and opportunities

  Being problem-led and focusing on daily challenges and opportunities
- [ ] Implementing the latest cutting-edge AI technologies

  While advanced AI technologies can be beneficial, the focus should be on solving real problems, not just implementing technology for technology's sake.
- [ ] Having a large budget for AI initiatives

  A large budget alone does not guarantee success if the AI projects do not address meaningful challenges.
- [x] Hiring a large team of AI experts

  Having skilled AI talent is important, but the size of the team is less crucial than ensuring the projects are problem-led.
::

Who is best positioned to identify high-impact, ready-to-implement AI projects in government?

- [x] External AI consultants

  While external consultants can provide valuable expertise, they may not have the same deep understanding of an organization's daily challenges as internal staff.
- [ ] Program and operations staff

  Program and operations staff are best positioned to identify high-impact, ready-to-implement AI projects because they have firsthand knowledge of the daily challenges and opportunities in their work
- [ ] Senior leadership

  Senior leadership sets strategic priorities but may not be as familiar with the day-to-day operational challenges that AI could address.
- [x] IT department

  The IT department plays a crucial role in implementing AI projects but may not have the domain expertise to identify the most impactful opportunities.
::
