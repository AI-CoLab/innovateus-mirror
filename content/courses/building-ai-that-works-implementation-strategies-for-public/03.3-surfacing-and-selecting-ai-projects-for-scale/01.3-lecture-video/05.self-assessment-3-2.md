Who can use generative AI to develop pilots and prototypes for AI projects?

- [x] Only data scientists and AI experts

  While data scientists and AI experts play a crucial role in AI development, generative AI tools are designed to be accessible to users without specialized technical skills.
- [ ] Only senior leadership

  Senior leadership should be involved in setting AI strategy and priorities, but they do not need to be the ones directly using generative AI tools.
- [ ] Any staff member, regardless of technical background

  One of the key benefits of generative AI is that it can be used by any staff member, regardless of their technical background. This democratization of AI enables a wider range of employees to contribute ideas, develop pilots, and create prototypes for potential AI projects
- [x] Only external AI consultants

  External consultants can provide valuable expertise, but generative AI empowers internal staff to develop their own pilots and prototypes.

What is the main purpose of Fast Field Scanning in the context of AI project identification?

- [x] To find AI solutions that can be directly copied and implemented without modification

  Fast Field Scanning is about learning from others' successes, not directly copying solutions. Each organization's context and needs are unique, so adaptation is usually necessary.
- [ ] To assess which AI solutions have been successful in other jurisdictions

  The main purpose of Fast Field Scanning is to quickly assess which AI solutions have been successful in other jurisdictions. By learning from the experiences and outcomes of similar organizations, government agencies can identify promising AI use cases and best practices
- [ ] To identify AI vendors and consultants to partner with

  While Fast Field Scanning can help identify potential partners, its primary purpose is to assess the solutions themselves, not the vendors or consultants behind them.
- [x] To determine which AI technologies are the most popular and trendy

  Popularity and trendiness are not reliable indicators of an AI solution's effectiveness or appropriateness for a given organization. Fast Field Scanning focuses on proven success, not hype.
::
