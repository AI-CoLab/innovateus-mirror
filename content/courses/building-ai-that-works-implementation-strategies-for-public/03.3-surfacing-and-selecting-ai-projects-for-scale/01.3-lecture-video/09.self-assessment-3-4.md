What is the main purpose of conducting pilot projects for AI initiatives?

- [x] To fully deploy the AI solution across the organization
- [ ] To test the feasibility, viability, and safety of the project on a small scale
- [ ] To eliminate the need for further testing and refinement of the AI solution
- [x] To showcase the capabilities of the AI technology to stakeholders

:::feedback{when="correct"}
The main purpose of conducting pilot projects for AI initiatives is to test the feasibility, viability, and safety of the project on a small scale before considering wider-scale deployments
:::

:::feedback{when="incorrect"}
Pilot projects provide an opportunity to validate the AI solution in a real-world context, identify potential issues or limitations, and gather feedback from users and stakeholders. They are not meant for full-scale deployment, elimination of further testing, or primarily as a showcase for the technology. Rather, they serve as a critical step in the responsible and iterative development of AI solutions in government.
:::
::
