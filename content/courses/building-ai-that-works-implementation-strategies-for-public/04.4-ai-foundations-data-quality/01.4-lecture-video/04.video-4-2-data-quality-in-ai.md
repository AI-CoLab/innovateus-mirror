# Video 4.2: Data Quality in AI

This video explores the impact of data quality issues on AI projects, highlighting how poor data quality can lead to wasted staff hours and wrong conclusions. It explains how AI systems are more sensitive to data quality problems and can propagate the impact of those problems more widely and rapidly compared to non-AI projects. The video also discusses how AI can amplify existing biases in the data, leading to discriminatory outcomes, and emphasizes the importance of addressing data quality issues to ensure responsible and ethical AI deployment.
