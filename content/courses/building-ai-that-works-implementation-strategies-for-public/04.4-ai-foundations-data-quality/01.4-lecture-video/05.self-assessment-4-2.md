::knowledge-check{kind="one"}
Why is labeled data essential for supervised learning in machine learning-based AI models?

- [x] Labeled data helps the model learn to make accurate predictions or decisions

  Labeled data is essential for training machine learning-based AI models because it helps the model learn to make accurate predictions or decisions.
- [ ] Labeled data is easier to collect than unlabeled data

  Labeled data is often more challenging to collect than unlabeled data, as it requires human annotation.
- [ ] Labeled data ensures the model's outputs are always unbiased

  While labeled data is important, it does not guarantee unbiased model outputs, as bias can still be present in the labels or the data itself.
- [x] Labeled data is required for all types of AI models

  Labeled data is particularly important for machine learning-based AI models, but not all types of AI models require labeled data.
::

Which of the following are potential consequences of AI models amplifying biases in data? Select all that apply.

- [x] Unfair treatment of individuals based on protected characteristic
- [ ] Perpetuation of historical inequities and discrimination
- [ ] Reduction in AI model accuracy and reliability
- [x] Increased public trust in AI-based decision-making

:::feedback{when="correct"}
The correct answers are A, B, and C. AI models amplifying biases in data can lead to unfair treatment of individuals based on protected characteristics, perpetuation of historical inequities and discrimination, and reduction in AI model accuracy and reliability.
:::

It does not increase public trust in AI-based decision-making.
