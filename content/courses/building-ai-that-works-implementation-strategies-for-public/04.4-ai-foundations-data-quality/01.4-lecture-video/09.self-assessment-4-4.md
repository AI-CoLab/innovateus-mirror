What is the benefit of focusing on key datasets for AI projects?

- [x] It allows the project team to ignore data quality issues

  Focusing on key datasets does not mean ignoring data quality issues; rather, it allows for targeted efforts to address quality issues in the most critical data.
- [ ] It ensures that all available data is used, regardless of relevance

  Focusing on key datasets involves prioritizing the most relevant data, not using all available data regardless of relevance.
- [ ] It enables targeted efforts to improve data quality for the most critical data

  The benefit of focusing on key datasets for AI projects is that it enables targeted efforts to improve data quality for the most critical data.
- [x] It eliminates the need for stakeholder involvement in data quality efforts

  Focusing on key datasets does not eliminate the need for stakeholder involvement; in fact, stakeholder alignment is crucial for ensuring data quality in these critical datasets.
