# Video 4.1: Six dimensions of data quality

This video introduces the critical role of data quality in AI projects, emphasizing that data is the fuel that powers AI models. It defines data quality in the public sector and explains why it is especially impactful for AI projects. The video also discusses the importance of having a realistic and specific understanding of data quality based on the six established dimensions: accuracy, consistency, completeness, uniqueness, timeliness, and validity.
