What distinguishes Generative AI (GenAI) from traditional machine learning?

- [x] A. GenAI is considered a form of Narrow AI, while traditional machine learning is a form of General Purpose AI
- [ ] B. GenAI is trained on smaller, more focused datasets compared to traditional machine learning
- [ ] C. GenAI can perform a wide variety of tasks using a single model, while traditional machine learning models are designed for specific tasks
- [ ] D. GenAI requires more technical expertise to use than traditional machine learning models

:::feedback{when="incorrect"}
The correct answer is C. The key distinction between Generative AI (GenAI) and traditional machine learning is that GenAI models can perform a wide variety of tasks using a single model, while traditional machine learning models are designed for specific, narrow tasks. GenAI is considered a form of General Purpose AI (A), and is trained on vast amounts of diverse data (B), making it more versatile than traditional machine learning. Additionally, GenAI models are often more accessible to non-technical users (D) due to their natural language interfaces, while traditional machine learning may require more technical expertise to use effectively.
