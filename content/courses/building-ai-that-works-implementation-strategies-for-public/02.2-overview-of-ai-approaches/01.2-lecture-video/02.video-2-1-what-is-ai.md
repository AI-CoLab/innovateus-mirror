# Video 2.1: What is AI

This video provides a jargon-free introduction to artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning, and generative AI. It explains how AI works by identifying patterns in large amounts of data to solve problems and highlights the distinctions between narrow AI and general-purpose AI. Examples of AI applications in government, such as healthcare and fraud detection, are presented to illustrate the potential impact of these technologies.
