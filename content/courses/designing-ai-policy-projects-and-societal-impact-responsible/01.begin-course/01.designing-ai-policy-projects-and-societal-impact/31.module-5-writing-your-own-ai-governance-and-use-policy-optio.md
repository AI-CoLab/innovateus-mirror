# Module 5: Writing your own AI governance and use policy (optional)

This optional module guides legal professionals on how to develop and implement an AI acceptable use policy for their office in line with best practices for data governance when using AI tools. This video provides guidance on creating a comprehensive AI governance policy for public sector legal offices. It covers three key aspects: setting the groundwork by identifying goals and priorities, developing core policy components including definitions and permitted uses, and implementing and evaluating the policy through pilots and training.
