Problem definition is a multi-step process of unpeeling the layers of onion. Done right - you will end up with a concise and clear statement of its essence, identifying the people who are affected by the problem, and an understanding of the root causes.The steps to defining a problem seem simple, but there are some common challenges:

- The constant temptation to articulate a solution rather than a problem.
- A problem definition that is too broad to be actionable and fails to pinpoint a specific community and geography to focus on in the short term will not work.
- The failure to articulate assumptions and root causes will also undermine your problem definition. While problems have many root causes, not all root causes are ones you can act on with the resources you have at your disposal.
