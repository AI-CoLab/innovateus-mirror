# Video 2.1: Why Problem Definition Matters

Albert Einstein famously said, “If I had only one hour to save the world, I would spend fifty-five minutes defining the problem, and only five minutes finding the solution.” If we are honest, most of us aren’t very rigorous in defining the problem we are trying to solve. This is especially true in the public sector, where we are under significant pressure to come up with solutions, rather than more problems. In this video, we will work together to learn the art and science of problem definition - the first and most essential skill of an effective public problem solver.
