# Readings: Problem Definition

Please find a few optional readings below to help enhance your understanding of the topic presented in this module.

- 📖  **[Defining and Analyzing the Problem](https://ctb.ku.edu/en/table-of-contents/analyze/analyze-community-problems-and-solutions/define-analyze-problem/main "Defining and Analyzing the Problem"),** Jenette Nagy, Chapter 17 in Analyzing Community Problems and Designing and Adapting Community Interventions. Community Tool Box

  In the “Defining and Analyzing the Problem” section of the Community Toolbox, readers will learn how to determine the nature of a problem, clarify the problem, decide to solve the problem, and analyze the problem with our processes. The toolkit includes readings, a checklist, examples, tools, and a PowerPoint presentation to reinforce the learnings on problem definition.

- **[📖 ](https://www.fastcompany.com/90649200/7-skills-you-need-if-you-want-to-solve-public-problems "📖 7 skills you need if you want to solve public problems") [Are You Solving the Right Problems?](https://hbr.org/2017/01/are-you-solving-the-right-problems "Are You Solving the Right Problems?")** Responsibly - Volume 1: Mirror, Mirror, Thomas Wedell-Wedellsborg, Harvard Business Review, January-February 2017

  Through over a decade of research into innovation, Thomas Wedell-Wedellsborg found that leaders of private and public sector organizations routinely struggle not with solving problems, but with figuring out what the problems are. In this article, the author outlines seven practices for effective reframing, which can ultimately lead to more creative solutions to the problems themselves.
