In this video, we’re going to spend more time on open data. What distinguishes open data from “administrative data” is that open data is publicly available, can be freely accessed and used, and is capable of being processed by a machine. Unlike administrative data, open data is generally population level, such as census records, environmental safety records, or workforce data, as opposed to individual-level data. Increasingly, governments at every level are publishing more data as open data.
