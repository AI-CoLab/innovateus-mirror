# Readings (Data Analytical Thinking)

Please find a few optional readings below to help enhance your understanding of the topic presented in this module.

- [📖](https://www.thersa.org/discover/publications-and-articles/reports/move-fast-and-fix-things "📖 Move fast and fix things: How to be a public entrepreneur,") [The Future of Data and Analytics, ](http://www.businessofgovernment.org/sites/default/files/Chapter%20Sixteen%20The%20Future%20of%20Data%20and%20Analytics.pdf)Shelley H. Metzenbaum. Chapter 16 in Government for the Future Reflection and Vision for Tomorrow's Leaders, 2018

  This report explores how federal, state, and local governments should use and communicate data and analytics to improve government performance across multiple dimensions—including impact, return on spending, fairness, interaction quality, trust and understanding

- [📖](https://www.thersa.org/discover/publications-and-articles/reports/move-fast-and-fix-things "📖 Move fast and fix things: How to be a public entrepreneur,") [Bit by Bit: Social Research in the Digital Age](https://www.bitbybitbook.com/), Matthew J. Salganik, August 6, 2019

  This is an innovative and accessible guide to doing social research in the digital age, providing a resource for social scientists who want to harness the research potential of big data and for data scientists interested in applying the lessons of social science to tomorrow's technologies.
