# Video 7.2: A Checklist for Equitable Engagement \[Part 2]

In this video, we talk about when to use public engagement, and why it’s important to put equity at the heart of your public engagement. To run a successful public engagement, it’s important to have a plan from the outset that identifies what you want to learn, whom you will engage, how you will engage them, and how you will use what they learn.

**Remember to answer the self assessment questions at the end of the video!**
