Please find a few optional readings below to help enhance your understanding of the topic presented in this module.

- 📖 **[Inclusive Community Engagement & Equitable Participation to Improve 4 Core Functions of Local Government](https://medium.com/changelab-solutions/equitable-community-engagement-34d2542f68fd "Inclusive Community Engagement & Equitable Participation to Improve 4 Core Functions of Local Government [Opens in new window]")** \[Opens in new window], Katie Hannon Michel, Cesar De La Vega, & Tina Yuen, ChangeLab Solutions, October 15, 2018

This blog post highlights 4 core functions of local government, examining how current “business as usual” efforts to engage the community can reinforce inequitable outcomes and offers promising strategies that advance more robust and inclusive community engagement and participation.

- 📖 **[Equitable Community Resilience Evaluation Toolkit Equitable Engagement Checklist](https://www.nj.gov/dep/bcrp/resilientnj/docs/equity-toolkit--engagement-checklist.pdf "Equitable Community Resilience Evaluation Toolkit Equitable Engagement Checklist</a> </strong>[Opens in new window]")** \[Opens in new window], Resilient NJ, accessed November 30, 2022

This engagement checklist will help users evaluate how equitable engagement opportunities are for different socially vulnerable populations throughout the various stages of resilience planning. The core principles of equitable planning are also provided as these underlying concepts are central to the recommendations in the provided checklist.
