# Readings (Human Centered Design)

Please find a few optional readings below to help enhance your understanding of the topic presented in this module.

- [📖 ](https://www.fastcompany.com/90649200/7-skills-you-need-if-you-want-to-solve-public-problems "📖 7 skills you need if you want to solve public problems") **[Designing for Public Services,](https://media.nesta.org.uk/documents/nesta_ideo_guide_jan2017.pdf)** Nesta and IDEO, January 2017

  In this guide by IDEO and Nesta, readers will be introduced to a process called design thinking and given a set of tools and methods that underpin it. Design thinking offers new ways to do things differently and this guide provides readers with guidance on how they can introduce the techniques into their day-to-day work in the public sector.

- [📖 ](https://www.fastcompany.com/90649200/7-skills-you-need-if-you-want-to-solve-public-problems "📖 7 skills you need if you want to solve public problems") [**Essay: Advanced Design for the Public Sector,**](https://medium.com/@jonathanveale/essay-advanced-design-for-the-public-sector-223ded08f7d9) Jonathan Veale, Medium, January 17, 2018

  This essay introduces the reader to leading thinkers in design, and lays out their contributions to the field of using design thinking for the public sector.

- [📖](https://www.fastcompany.com/90649200/7-skills-you-need-if-you-want-to-solve-public-problems "📖 7 skills you need if you want to solve public problems")  [**A Collection of Tools to Bring Human-Centered Design into Your Project**, 18F Methods (website), accessed ](https://methods.18f.gov/)November 28, 2022

  This website offers a collection of tools to bring human-centered design into people’s projects. Readers can use the Discover Methods to build context for the problem they’re investigating, to get to know their potential users better, and understand their stakeholders’ main concerns.

- **[📖](https://www.fastcompany.com/90649200/7-skills-you-need-if-you-want-to-solve-public-problems "📖 7 skills you need if you want to solve public problems")  [Universal Methods of Design](https://books.google.com/books/about/Universal_Methods_of_Design.html?id=pCVATIpzYfUC),** Bruce Hanington and Bella Martin, Universal Methods of Design, 2012

  This comprehensive reference provides a thorough and critical presentation of 100 research methods, synthesis/analysis techniques, and research deliverables for human centered design, delivered in a concise and accessible format perfect for designers, educators, and students.
