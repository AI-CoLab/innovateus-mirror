# Video 3.1: Why is Human Centered Design Important?

In this video, we’ll continue our discussion about problem definition with strategies for deepening your understanding of the problem by asking the people who are most affected. These strategies are known as human-centered design or design thinking. Human-centered design (HCD) is a structured set of methods and tools for consulting with residents, who are the target audience for your policy or service. The goal is to understand how the residents perceive the problem to be solved.
