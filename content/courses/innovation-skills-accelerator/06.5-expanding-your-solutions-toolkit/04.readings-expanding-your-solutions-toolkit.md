# Readings (Expanding Your Solutions Toolkit)

The following readings on Expanding Your Solutions Toolkit deepen what we have learned in this module. Please be sure to check out the Worksheets for hands-on exercises to apply the lessons learned to your own projects.

- **[📖  Tom Kalil: Leading Technology & Innovation at the White House ](http://store.hbr.org/product/tom-kalil-leading-technology-innovation-at-the-white-house/417021 "Tom Kalil: Leading Technology & Innovation at the White House")**\[Opens in a new window] Hill, Linda A., and Allison J. Wigen, Harvard Business School Case 417-021, August 2016. (Revised March 2019.)

This case explores the role of Tom Kalil as Deputy Director for Technology & Innovation at the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy and discusses leading innovation ecosystems; building public-private and public-public collaborations; leading system innovation; talent management and development; and public sector innovation.

- **[📖  Toolkit Navigator: A compendium of toolkits for public sector innovation and transformation ](https://oecd-opsi.org/toolkit-navigator/ "Toolkit Navigator: A compendium of toolkits for public sector innovation and transformation")**\[Opens in a new window], OECD, Observatory of Public Sector Innovation, accessed November 28, 2022

OPSI’s Toolkit Navigator is a compendium of toolkits for public sector innovation and transformation. It provides a pathway to hundreds of freely available innovation toolkits, developed by authors in the public, private, academic and not-for-profit sectors, including information about common methodologies used for public sector innovation, links to relevant government case studies applying these methodologies and access to a network of public sector innovators.
