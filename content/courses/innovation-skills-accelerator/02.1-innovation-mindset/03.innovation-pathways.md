# Innovation Pathways

In this short video, we introduce you to new tools, methods, and ways of thinking that can help you to address today’s problems in ways that are more agile, effective, and equitable. We’ll walk you through the steps required to take a solution from idea to implementation. We want to spark new thinking about ways that we as public servants can better deliver services, design policies and serve the public.
