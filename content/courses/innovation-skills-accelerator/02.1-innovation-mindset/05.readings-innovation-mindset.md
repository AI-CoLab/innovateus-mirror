The following readings on Innovation Pathway deepen what we have learned in this module. Please be sure to check out the Worksheets for hands-on exercises to apply the lessons learned to your own projects.

- [📖 ](https://www.thersa.org/discover/publications-and-articles/reports/move-fast-and-fix-things "📖 Move fast and fix things: How to be a public entrepreneur,")[**Move fast and fix things: How to be a public entrepreneur** *\[Opens in a new window\],* ](https://www.thersa.org/discover/publications-and-articles/reports/move-fast-and-fix-things)Rowan Conway, Luke Timmons, Ian Burbidge, RSA Lab, July 24, 2018 (Chapters 1 and 2)

  This report describes the findings from a six-month inquiry into applying the “think like a system, act like an entrepreneur” mentality to the challenges of procuring and scaling innovation through government. Through qualitative workshops, deep dives into case studies around the UK and a global practice review, the authors unearthed examples where practitioners have acted entrepreneurially to enable the success of enterprise innovations.

- [📖 ](https://www.fastcompany.com/90649200/7-skills-you-need-if-you-want-to-solve-public-problems "📖 7 skills you need if you want to solve public problems")[**7 skills you need if you want to solve public problems** *\[Opens in a new window\]*](https://www.fastcompany.com/90649200/7-skills-you-need-if-you-want-to-solve-public-problems "7 skills you need if you want to solve public problems [Opens in a new window]"), Beth Simone Noveck, Fast Company, June 24, 2021

  This article, which draws from author Beck Noveck’s book, “Solving Public Problems: A Practical Guide to Fix Our Government and Change Our World,” explains the seven skills public problem solvers need to develop in order to change policy to improve people’s lives.
