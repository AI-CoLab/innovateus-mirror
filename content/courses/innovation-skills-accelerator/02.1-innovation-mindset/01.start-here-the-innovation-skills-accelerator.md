# Start here: The Innovation Skills Accelerator

👋 Thank you for signing up to participate in the Innovation Skills Accelerator.

👥 Before you begin, please take a moment to review your learner profile and make sure to update your first and last name as you'd like it to appear in your profile and course completion certificate. You can review and update your details by clicking [this link](https://course.innovate-us.org/user/edit.php).

💡 This introductory online course will provide you with the digital, data and innovation skills to deliver more effective, equitable and engaged policies and services.

👩‍🎓Through videos, self-assessments and worksheets, you will engage in practical lessons learned about improved ways of working.

🕐 The course will be released in three parts and the first part of the course is available for you to start learning. The other two will be released in separate phases that you will be notified of.

📖 The course consists of 15 modules, which include short videos, optional readings and videos led by expert faculty members in their respective fields.

🗓️ You can participate in this course at your own pace and on your own schedule.

✅ If you complete the course you will receive a Certificate of Completion, which may be applied to state learning requirements.
