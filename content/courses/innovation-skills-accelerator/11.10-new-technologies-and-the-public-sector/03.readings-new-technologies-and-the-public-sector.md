Please find a few optional readings below to help enhance your understanding of the topic presented in this module.

- 📖 [**Digital Government: Strategy, Government Models and Technology** ](https://thelivinglib.org/digital-government-strategy-government-models-and-technology/ "Digital Government: Strategy, Government Models and Technology [Opens in a new window]")\[Opens in new window], Bernd W. Wirtz, 2022

This textbook introduces the concept of digital government as well as digital management and provides helpful insights and strategic advice for the successful implementation and maintenance of digital government systems.

- 📖 **[Digital Government Toolkit](https://www.oecd.org/governance/digital-government/toolkit/home/ "Digital Government Toolkit [Opens in a new window]")** \[Opens in new window], OECD

This OECD toolkit is designed to help readers implement the OECD Recommendation on Digital Government Strategies. By comparing good practices across OECD countries, this site can guide decision-makers in using digital technologies to encourage innovation, transparency, and efficiency in the public sector.
