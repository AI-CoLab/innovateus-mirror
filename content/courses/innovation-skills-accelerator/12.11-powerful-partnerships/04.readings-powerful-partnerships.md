# Readings (Powerful Partnerships)

The following readings on Powerful Partnerships deepen what we have learned in this module. Please be sure to check out the Worksheets for hands-on exercises to apply the lessons learned to your own projects.

- **[📖  The Intersector Toolkit,](http://intersector.com/toolkit/ "The Intersector Toolkit")**\[Opens in a new window] The Intersector Project (accessed November 28, 2022).

This toolkit is a guide to help diagnose, design, implement, and assess successful cross-sector collaborations. Practitioners from any sector can use these tools to navigate their challenges.

- **[📖 The Essential Skills of Cross Sector Leadership, ](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/the_essential_skills_of_cross_sector_leadership# "The Essential Skills of Cross Sector Leadership")**\[Opens in a new window] David B. Smith and Jeanine Becker, Stanford Social Innovation Review, Winter 2018.

This report offers lessons learned on the essential skills required for cross-sector leadership in the areas of building teams, solving problems, and achieving impact.

- **[📖  The SDG Partnership Guidebook](https://sdgs.un.org/sites/default/files/2022-02/SDG%20Partnership%20Guidebook%201.11.pdf)[,](https://www.thepartneringinitiative.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/SDG-Partnership-Guidebook-1.0.pdf "The SDG Partnership Guidebook")**\[Opens in a new window] Darian Stibbe and Dave Prescott, The Partnering Initiative and UNDESA, 2020

The United Nations and The Partnering Initiative offer this practical guide to building high impact multi-stakeholder partnerships for the Sustainable Development Goals.
