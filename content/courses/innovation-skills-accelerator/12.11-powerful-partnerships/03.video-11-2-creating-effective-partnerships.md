11.2 Creating Effective Partnerships

[Next: Readings](https://course.innovate-us.org/mod/page/view.php?id=327)

We’ll review six basic steps in creating effective partnerships.

- - Assess whether partners are needed
  - Create clear plan and implementation checklist
  - Identify partners
  - Reach out to partners
  - Define common goals and vision of success
  - Create an accountability mechanism
