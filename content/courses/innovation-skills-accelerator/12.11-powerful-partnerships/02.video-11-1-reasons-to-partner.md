# Video 11.1 Reasons to Partner

Let's face it, most of us–especially in the public sector–rarely have all the resources we need: we sometimes lack money (or the ability to spend it easily), we lack talent such as programmers, data scientists, and marketers, and, above all, we lack time! All these requirements are good reasons for finding partners. But it can be hard to know how to partner efficiently and in a way that safeguards the public interest. That’s why in  this module, you will learn:

- The advantages of partnering to overcome implementation challenges.

- The how-to’s of partnership and collaboration. We’ll provide you with practical worksheets and guidance.

- The risks of partnering and how to mitigate them.
