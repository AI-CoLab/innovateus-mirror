# Video 14.1: Introduction to Pilots

Even if your chosen solution has been tried elsewhere before, you may be trying this solution for the first time. You'll want to gather data to show whether your efforts are working in your own unique context. That’s why we provide you with an introduction to

- - why pilots are beneficial
  - how to design a pilot
  - and the pitfalls of piloting and how to overcome them
