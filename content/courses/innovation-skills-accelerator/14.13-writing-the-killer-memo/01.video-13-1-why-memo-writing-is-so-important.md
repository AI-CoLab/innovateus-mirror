# Video 13.1: Why Memo Writing is So Important

Writing a memo in a disciplined way can also guide you to express your idea clearly and communicate it effectively to multiple audiences. By the end of this module, you will

- - Understand the importance of good memo writing

  - Learn tricks and strategies of how to write a persuasive memo

  - Know some resources for sample memos, worksheets, and materials to further help you learn to write a good memo
