Please find a few optional readings below to help enhance your understanding of the topic presented in this module.

- **[📖  Policy Memos ](https://shorensteincenter.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/07/HO_Herman_Policy-Memos_9_24_12.pdf "Policy Memos[Opens in a new window]")**\[Opens in a new window], Harvard Kennedy School, Fall 2012

These guidelines teach the strategies, mechanics, and structure of a basic policy memo.

- \

  [📖 **The Policy Memo, A How-To Guide, (presentation)** ](https://scholar.princeton.edu/sites/default/files/ccameron/files/the_policy_memo_2016.pdf "The Policy Memo, A How-To Guide, (presentation) [Opens in a new window]")\[Opens in a new window], Princeton University, accessed November 28, 2022

This presentation lays out clear and comprehensive tips for writing a good policy memo.

- \

  [📖 **Eight Tips for Communicating Ideas to Busy Policymakers** ](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/eight_tips_for_communicating_ideas_to_busy_policymakers "Eight Tips for Communicating Ideas to Busy Policymakers [Opens in a new window]")\[Opens in a new window], Lisa Witter and Odette Chalaby, Stanford Social Innovation Review, December 3, 2018

This article lays out eight tips for effective communication with policymakers and decisionmakers, which are a helpful accompaniment in the process of writing a policy memo.
