In this video, we continue to discuss fast field scanning, a set of methods for rapidly identifying solutions, determining whether those solutions worked and assessing collaborative whether what worked elsewhere might solve the problem in your community. We’ve cover strategies that involve using documentary sources and talking to people in your search for information. We also discuss how systematic reviews and collective intelligence can be used to evaluate evidence.
