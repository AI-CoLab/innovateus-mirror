# Video 8.1: Introduction to Fast Field Scanning

Using the power of the crowd is only one way to find solutions. Let’s talk about fast field scanning and scavenging for solutions quickly. Today we’ll talk about:

- How to find solutions that have already been implemented elsewhere.
- How to evaluate whether those existing solutions worked.
- How to determine whether what worked "there" will also work "here," in your context and community.
