Please find a few optional readings below to help enhance your understanding of the topic presented in this module.

- **[📖  Logic Model Development Guide ](https://www.naccho.org/uploads/downloadable-resources/Programs/Public-Health-Infrastructure/KelloggLogicModelGuide_161122_162808.pdf "Logic Model Development Guide [Opens in a new window]")**\[Opens in a new window], W. K. Kellogg Foundation, January 1, 2004

This guide was developed to provide practical assistance to nonprofits engaged looking to create outcome-oriented evaluation of their projects. The guide is intended to provide sufficient orientation to the underlying principles of "logic modeling" to use this tool to enhance their program planning, implementation, and dissemination activities.

- **[📖  In Defense of Logic Models ](https://www.huffpost.com/entry/in-defense-of-logic-model_b_1633676 "In Defense of Logic Models [Opens in a new window]")**\[Opens in a new window], Ian David Moss, Huffington Post, July 5, 2012, updated December 6, 2017

In this article, author Ian David Moss explains what a logic model is and lays out the case for creating a logic model in pursuit of the principles of articulation, flexibility, and transparency.
