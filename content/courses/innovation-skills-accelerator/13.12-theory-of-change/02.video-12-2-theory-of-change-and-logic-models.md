# Video 12.2 Theory of Change and Logic Models

Funders often will ask you to present a theory of change, so it is a good discipline to develop. The theory of change, or logic model, is another way of asking you to define your problem and to explain how and why  your solution actually solves that problem. This may sound easy in theory, but it is actually quite challenging.
