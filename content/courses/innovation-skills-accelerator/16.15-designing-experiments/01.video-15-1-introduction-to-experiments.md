# Video 15.1: Introduction to Experiments

What can you do when you want to measure if your pilot--and later your initiative-- is working? This module will help you:

- - Explore various methods for testing and evaluating an intervention, especially more participatory and democratic methods of evaluation;
  - Understand the limits and challenges of different evaluation methods; and
  - Understand which method is most suitable for your needs.
