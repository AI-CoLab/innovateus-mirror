# Video 9.2: Designing Your Own Behavioral Insights Program

In this video, we’ll focus on one particular type of solution: - behavioral insights - to illustrate how findings from social and behavioral sciences, such as psychology and economics, can improve the design of public policies, programs, and services. After completing this session, we hope you will understand how public servants can apply behavioral insights to our work.
