# Readings (Collective Intelligence)

Please find a few optional readings below to help enhance your understanding of the topic presented in this module.

- **[📖  Collective Intelligence: A Checklist for the Public Sector ](https://media.nesta.org.uk/documents/Collective_Intelligence_A_checklist_for_the_public_sector.pdf "Collective Intelligence: A Checklist for the Public Sector [Opens in a new window]")**\[Opens in a new window], Matt Ryan, Dane Gambrell, Beth Simone Noveck, October 2020

This guide outlines a simple checklist to help institutions interested in making the most of the opportunities in collective intelligence. The checklist is phrased as a series of questions that every public sector practitioner should answer to make sure they don’t miss any key elements of designing a CI initiative.

- [📖 **The GovLab Collective Intelligence Podcast** ](https://anchor.fm/thegovlab "The GovLab Collective Intelligence Podcast [Opens in a new window]")\[Opens in a new window], The GovLab

The GovLab Collective Intelligence Podcast contains interviews with experts to tell the amazing story of the ways that cities are solving problems with citizens.
