::knowledge-check{kind="several"}
Which of the following statements are true about the role of AI in government projects? Select all that apply.

- [x] AI should be used in every government project, regardless of its applicability
- [ ] AI is just one of many tools that can be used to improve government services
- [ ] The success of a government project should be measured by its impact on residents, not its use of AI
- [x] Government organizations should always prioritize AI-driven solutions over non-AI alternatives
- [ ] Identifying useful non-AI projects through the AI project selection process is a valuable outcome

:::feedback{when="correct"}
The correct answers are B, C, and E. AI is just one of many tools that can be used to improve government services, and its use should be guided by a thoughtful consideration of each project's unique needs and goals. The success of a government project should be measured by its impact on residents, not its use of AI. If the AI project selection process identifies useful non-AI projects, that is a valuable outcome that should be celebrated and pursued.
:::

:::feedback{when="incorrect"}
Statements that suggest using AI in every project, prioritizing AI-driven solutions over non-AI alternatives, or measuring success solely by the use of AI are misguided and can lead to suboptimal outcomes for government organizations and the citizens they serve.
:::
::

::knowledge-check{kind="one"}
What is the main purpose of conducting pilot projects for AI initiatives?

- [x] To fully deploy the AI solution across the organization

  Pilot projects are not intended for full-scale deployment, but rather for initial testing and validation in a controlled environment.
- [ ] To test the feasibility, viability, and safety of the project on a small scale

  The main purpose of conducting pilot projects for AI initiatives is to test the feasibility, viability, and safety of the project on a small scale before considering wider-scale deployments.
- [ ] To eliminate the need for further testing and refinement of the AI solution

  Pilot projects do not eliminate the need for further testing and refinement, but rather provide valuable insights and feedback to inform the ongoing development and improvement of the AI solution.
- [x] To showcase the capabilities of the AI technology to stakeholders

  While pilot projects can help demonstrate the potential of AI to stakeholders, their primary purpose is to assess the technical, operational, and safety aspects of the solution, not to serve as a showcase.
::
