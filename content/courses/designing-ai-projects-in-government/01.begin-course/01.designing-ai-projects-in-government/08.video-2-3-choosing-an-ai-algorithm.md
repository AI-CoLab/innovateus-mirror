# Video 2.3: Choosing an AI algorithm

This video delves into the crucial decision of choosing the right algorithm for an AI project, highlighting the trade-off between performance and interpretability. It explains how interpretability is essential in the public sector, where decisions directly impact residents' lives. The video emphasizes the importance of collaborating with data scientists, technologists, and AI ethicists to ensure the chosen approach aligns with the project's requirements and potential risks.
