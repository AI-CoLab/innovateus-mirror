::knowledge-check{kind="one"}
What is a key benefit of using a more interpretable AI model, for high-stakes decisions?

- [x] A. Increased efficiency and reduced computational costs
- [ ] B. Improved ability to identify and mitigate bias in the model's predictions
- [ ] C. Greater protection against adversarial attacks and security breaches
- [ ] D. Enhanced public trust and accountability through transparent decision-making processes

:::feedback{when="incorrect"}
The correct answer is D. A key benefit of using a more interpretable model for high-stakes decisions is enhanced public trust and accountability, as the transparent decision-making process allows for greater scrutiny and understanding of how the model arrives at its predictions. While interpretability is important, it does not necessarily improve efficiency (A), mitigate bias (B), or protect against security threats (C) in the ways suggested by the other options. These aspects of model performance and robustness are influenced more by factors such as the model's architecture, training data, and security practices.
:::
::

::knowledge-check{kind="several"}
Which of the following are potential drawbacks of using deep learning models for decision-making in the public sector? Select all that apply.

- [x] A. Lack of transparency in how the model arrives at its decisions
- [ ] B. Increased risk of the model learning and perpetuating biases from the training data
- [ ] C. Difficulty in achieving high accuracy and performance on complex tasks
- [ ] D. High computational costs and longer training times compared to simpler models

:::feedback{when="incorrect"}
The correct answers are A, B, and D. Potential drawbacks of using deep learning models for public sector decision-making include lack of transparency in the model's decision-making process (A), increased risk of the model learning and amplifying biases from the training data (B), and high computational costs and longer training times compared to simpler models (D). While deep learning models can struggle with interpretability and bias, they are often able to achieve high accuracy on complex tasks (C), making this option incorrect in the context of the question.
:::
::

::knowledge-check{kind="one"}
What is the main reason for using deep learning models despite their potential drawbacks?

- [x] A. Deep learning models are more transparent and explainable than other types of models
- [ ] B. Deep learning models can achieve high performance on complex tasks that other models struggle with
- [ ] C. Deep learning models are less computationally expensive and faster to train than simpler models
- [ ] D. Deep learning models are more resistant to biases in the training data compared to other models

:::feedback{when="incorrect"}
The correct answer is B. The main reason for using deep learning models despite their potential drawbacks is their ability to achieve high performance on complex tasks, such as image recognition, natural language processing, and predicting intricate outcomes, that other models may struggle with. The transparency (A), computational efficiency (C), and bias resistance (D) mentioned in the other options are not the primary motivations for using deep learning models and are actually potential drawbacks that need to be carefully considered and mitigated when deploying these models in practice.
:::
