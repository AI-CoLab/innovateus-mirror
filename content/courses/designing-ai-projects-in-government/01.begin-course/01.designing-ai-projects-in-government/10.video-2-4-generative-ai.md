# Video 2.4: Generative AI

This video focuses on the differences between generative AI and traditional machine learning. It explains how generative AI is a general-purpose AI that can perform a wide variety of tasks, while traditional machine learning is designed for specific tasks. The video also discusses the accessibility of generative AI, which allows users to interact using plain language, and the importance of fine-tuning models for specialized tasks. Additionally, it covers the challenges of generative AI, such as lack of interpretability and the potential to replicate biases found in training data.
