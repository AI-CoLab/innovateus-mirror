# Video 1.1 | Course Overview

The course introduction highlights the potential of Generative AI (GenAI) to revolutionize work in the public sector. Participants will learn to use GenAI as a powerful companion to assist in their work through hands-on activities and best practices. The importance of delivering value while maintaining public trust and safety is emphasized.

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

Explain in plain language what Generative AI is and how it works

Apply GenAI to ten types of tasks common in public sector work

Write effective prompts using proven techniques
