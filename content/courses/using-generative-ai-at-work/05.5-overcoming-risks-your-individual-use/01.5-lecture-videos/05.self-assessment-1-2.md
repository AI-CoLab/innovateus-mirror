What is the term used to describe the phenomenon of generative AI tools producing false or inaccurate information?

- [x] Deception

  Deception implies intentional misleading, which is not the case with generative AI hallucinations.
- [ ] Hallucination
- [ ] Bias

  While bias can contribute to inaccurate outputs, the specific phenomenon described here is known as hallucination.
- [x] Glitch

  A glitch suggests a temporary malfunction, whereas hallucination is a byproduct of the AI's language modeling approach.

What is the primary reason generative AI tools can sometimes produce false or inaccurate information?

- [x] They intentionally deceive users by generating false information

  Generative AI tools do not intentionally deceive users, but can produce inaccurate information due to their probabilistic nature.
- [ ] They generate responses based on patterns in language, which can produce text that sounds convincing but is actually false
- [ ] They are programmed to occasionally generate false information to test user attentiveness

  False information is not intentionally generated to test users, but is a byproduct of the AI's language modeling approach.
- [x] They have a built-in bias towards generating false information

  The generation of false information is not due to a built-in bias, but rather a result of the probabilistic language modeling technique.
::

::knowledge-check{kind="one"}
Why is using more than one AI model helpful for spotting hallucinations?

- [x] Different models will always produce the same hallucinations

  Different models are likely to produce different hallucinations, not the same ones.
- [ ] Comparing outputs from multiple models can highlight inconsistencies and potential inaccuracies
- [ ] Using multiple models guarantees the complete elimination of hallucinations

  While using multiple models can help identify hallucinations, it does not guarantee their complete elimination.
- [x] Multiple models are required to generate any output at all

  Multiple models are not required to generate output, but rather serve as a means of comparison to identify potential issues.
::

What is the most important step when using generative AI tools?

- [x] Blindly trusting the AI-generated output without review

  Blindly trusting AI output without review can lead to the use of inaccurate or inappropriate information.
- [ ] Checking and proofreading the results generated by the AI
- [ ] Assuming the AI output is always inferior to human-generated content

  While human oversight is important, assuming AI output is always inferior can lead to underutilization of these tools.
- [x] Avoiding the use of generative AI tools in all circumstances

  Generative AI tools can be helpful when used responsibly, so avoiding them altogether may not be the best approach.
