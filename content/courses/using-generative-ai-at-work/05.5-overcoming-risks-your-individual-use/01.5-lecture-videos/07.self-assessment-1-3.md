What does it mean when we say that generative AI "reflects the bias that created it"?

- [x] The AI tools actively create new biases that did not exist before

  Generative AI tools do not create entirely new biases, but rather reflect and perpetuate existing ones.
- [ ] The AI learns patterns from its training data and can reproduce or even amplify biases present in that data
- [ ] The AI tools eliminate all biases and produce perfectly neutral outputs

  While AI tools can be helpful, they are not capable of eliminating all biases and producing perfectly neutral outputs.
- [x] The AI tools only reflect the biases of their current users, not their training data

  The biases in AI outputs are primarily a reflection of the training data, not just the biases of current users.
::

::knowledge-check{kind="one"}
What makes it challenging to identify bias in generative AI outputs?

- [x] The transparent and easily understandable nature of AI decision-making processes

  AI decision-making processes are often complex and opaque, not transparent and easily understandable.
- [ ] The lack of bias in the data used to train AI models

  Bias in AI outputs often stems from biases present in the training data, not a lack of bias in the data.
- [ ] The complexity and unclear nature of AI decision-making processes
- [x] The absence of any bias in AI-generated outputs

  Bias can exist in AI-generated outputs, and the challenge lies in identifying and addressing it.
::

::knowledge-check{kind="one"}
What is an example of a task that you should be cautious about asking generative AI to perform?

- [x] Generating a summary of a publicly available research article

  Summarizing a publicly available research article is generally a low-risk task for AI, as long as the output is reviewed for accuracy.
- [ ] Producing a transcript of a public meeting recording

  Transcribing a public meeting recording is typically a suitable task for AI, provided no sensitive information is shared.
- [ ] Making a decision or recommendation that significantly impacts people's lives
- [x] Creating a first draft of a non-sensitive document for internal review

  Generating a first draft of a non-sensitive document for internal review is an appropriate use of AI, as long as the output is reviewed and edited by humans.
::
