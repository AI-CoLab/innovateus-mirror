# Using Generative AI at Work

This course provides public sector professionals with a comprehensive understanding of Generative AI (GenAI) and its potential to revolutionize work in government. Through hands-on activities and best practices, participants learn to use GenAI as a powerful companion to assist in their daily tasks while maintaining public trust and safety. The course covers the fundamentals of GenAI, its practical applications, prompt engineering techniques, risk mitigation strategies, and broader societal challenges. By the end of the course, participants will be equipped to harness the power of GenAI responsibly and effectively in their public sector roles.
