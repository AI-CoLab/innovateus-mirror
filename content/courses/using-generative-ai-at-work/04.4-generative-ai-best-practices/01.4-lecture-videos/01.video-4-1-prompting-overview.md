Having explored ways to use Generative AI, let’s turn to how to give it effective instructions. Generative AI works through plain-language directions—known as prompts. Crafting those instructions effectively is often called prompt engineering.

*\[Visual description]:* A theatre with a computer monitor quoting Shakespeare while it asks its director its next line. She responds: “So you want a prompt?”

GenAI’s effectiveness depends on how you use it. It often feels impressive on unfamiliar topics, but less so where you have expertise and can spot the gaps. Better prompting can help you to fill many of those gaps.
