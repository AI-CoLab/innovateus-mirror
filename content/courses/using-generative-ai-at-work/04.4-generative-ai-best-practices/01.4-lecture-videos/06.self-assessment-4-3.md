What should you do if a task is too complex for the AI to handle effectively?

- [x] Provide the AI with less information and context

  Providing less information and context can make the task more difficult for the AI, not easier.
- [ ] Divide the task into smaller, more manageable stages
- [ ] Give the AI more complex and detailed instructions

  Giving more complex instructions can further complicate the task for the AI, not simplify it.
- [x] Avoid using the AI for complex tasks altogether

  Breaking the task into stages allows the AI to assist with complex tasks, not avoid them altogether.
::
