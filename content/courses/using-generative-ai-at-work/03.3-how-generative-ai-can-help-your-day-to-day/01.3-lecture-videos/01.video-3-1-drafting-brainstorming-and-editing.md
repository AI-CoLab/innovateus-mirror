# Video 3.1 | Drafting, Brainstorming and Editing

This video explores three of the ten key areas where GenAI can assist: drafting content, brainstorming ideas, proofreading and editing. Hands-on demos and exercises allow participants to practice these applications.

Remember: human oversight is always crucial. Always review and fact-check GenAI-generated content. Research has shown that people all too often follow GenAI’s suggestions without critical scrutiny, even when it's wrong.

Let's explore ten common tasks we can now do more easily when we know how to use AI well.

*\[Visual description]:* Title card: 1: Drafting.

Facing a daunting blank page? Let GenAI kick-start your writing.

*\[Visual description]:* Text appears: Disclaimer: The commercial tools mentioned in this course are for illustrative purposes and do not imply endorsement by InnovateUS.

Whether it's a press release, policy memo, job description, website content, or phone tree responses, GenAI can produce a preliminary draft when given a bit of direction.

*\[Visual description]:* An AI tool is being asked to generate a formal email draft to invite members of the public to a government hearing.

GenAI can also create a first draft of an image — for example, an urban planning illustration for your event blog post or presentation.

*\[Visual description]:* The AI generates an image showing a group of people meeting while standing over a map model with information on the government hearing.

*\[Visual description]:* Title card: 2: Brainstorming.

Stuck on a project or a section of a policy memo? GenAI can play the role of brainstormer. Unlike a simple web search, you can have a real conversation with GenAI — generate ideas, refine them, and keep pushing until you find something useful. Remember: if you don't like the first response, keep prompting. You can have a dialogue — push back, ask for more, ask it to narrow down.

*\[Visual description]:* The AI generates responses to the asked questions and keeps up the conversation to help the user.

*\[Visual description]:* Title card: 2: Editing & Proofreading.

GenAI can catch errors and help you improve the clarity of your writing and the strength of your argument. It can help you correct your grammar, propose structural changes, or rewrite text, for example, for web pages or press releases.

*\[Visual description]:* An AI tool is being tasked to rewrite a paragraph of a memo to heighten the clarity and to make the text accessible for a 9th-grade reading level.

Remember if practicing with free tools, only use publicly available content.

*\[Visual description]:* Text appears: Warning. Do not include any personally identifiable information (PII) or sensitive information in your request.
