::knowledge-check{kind="several"}
When using generative AI to draft or modify content, what are the key responsibilities of the human user? (Select all that apply)

- [x] A. Reviewing the AI-generated content for accuracy
- [ ] B. Fact-checking the information provided by the AI
- [ ] C. Disclosing the use of AI in their work
- [x] D. Accepting the AI-generated content without modification

:::feedback{when="incorrect"}
The correct answers are A, B, and C. When using generative AI to draft or modify content, the key responsibilities of the human user include reviewing the AI-generated content for accuracy, fact-checking the information provided by the AI, and disclosing the use of AI in their work. Users should not accept the AI-generated content without review and potential modification.
:::
