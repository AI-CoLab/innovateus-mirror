# Video 4.2 | Five Parts of a Good Prompt

This video introduces a prompting framework with five components: Instruction, Role, Context, Format, and Example. Iterative prompt refinement is demonstrated through examples.

*\[Visual description]:* Title card: Understanding Risks.

A good prompt has five parts: Instruction, Role, Context, Format, and Example.

*\[Visual description]:* Title card: The five parts in action.

Instruction is the basic command you give to GenAI — like "write a report" or "summarize this."

*\[Visual description]:* Text appears: Instruction: Write a memo on this house hearing on the benefits of plain language in paperwork for businesses.

Just like an intern needs more direction, GenAI performs better with more specific guidance.

*\[Visual description]:* An AI writes the requested memo.

*\[Visual description]:* Text appears: Disclaimer: The commercial tools mentioned in this course are for illustrative purposes and do not imply endorsement by InnovateUS.

Role defines the persona you want GenAI to adopt — "friendly coworker," "tough mentor," "professional writer." This sets the tone and style of the output.

*\[Visual description]:* Text appears: Role: You are a concise and diligent professional writer.

*\[Visual description]:* An AI rewrites the memo now using language and style indicative of the role.

The more relevant details you provide, the better the output. So if you're asking GenAI to write a memo, give it context: tell it who the audience is, what their reading level is, or what themes will resonate with them.

*\[Visual description]:* A user adds information such as who it is written for, what that person is looking for, how in-depth the explanation should go, and what data should be included in the response.

Format specifies how you want the information presented. A bulleted list? A table? A narrative report?  Tell GenAI how to structure its response.

*\[Visual description]:* Text appears: Format: The report should include the following sections: Introduction, List of speakers, Core points and recommendations, areas of agreement, Areas of differences, and Conclusion. It should be no more than 200 words. Use bullet points where appropriate.

*\[Visual description]:* An AI rewrites a response after being given a format for the requested report.

You can even upload a sample of an old memo you liked and ask it to match the format and tone.

*\[Visual description]:* Text appears: Example: Use the below prior report as a guide for structure and detail level.

Let’s take an example: we'll start with a naked instruction first and iteratively add role, context, and format as we draft a memo on the benefits of plain language paperwork for small businesses.  By the end, you'll see a significant improvement.

A few principles for effective prompting:

Start simple: a few clear bullet points are often enough—you don’t need mini-essays

Clarity over formality: prompts don’t need to be polished, just understandable

Use components flexibly by combining instruction, role, context, and format as needed

Results improve as you clarify and build on previous prompts within a conversation

Then adjust based on results, and share useful prompts with colleagues

Now try it for yourself on an approved platform. For example, you might try drafting a job listing and iteratively improving your prompt to see how the output changes.
