### Audio description transcript - Conversational, Coding, and Data Analysis Uses

*\[Visual description]:* Text appears: The InnovateUS Logo.

*\[Visual description]:* Title card: Responsible AI for Public Professionals: Using GenAI in Government.

*\[Visual description]:* Title card: Conversational, Coding, and Data Analysis Uses.

*\[Visual description]:* Text appears: Ariana Escanlante, presenter, InnovateUS.

Welcome back! In our discussion of ten ways you can use GenAI, we've covered content creation and content analysis.

*\[Visual description]:* Content Creation is shown as a woman sitting behind a computer streaming out a torrent of music notes, scripts, and videos. Content Analysis is shown as a torrent of creative information being funneled into a lightbulb held by a woman.

But these tools are also great at teaching, especially when we use them to ask us questions.

*\[Visual description]: An image of an AI tutor breaking down a math problem to teach a user.*

*\[Visual description]:* Title card: 7: Role Playing.

GenAI can assist by adopting a role.

*\[Visual description]:* Title card: Sample Prompt.

*\[Visual description]:* The following prompt is presented to an AI: "I have a public event coming up where I will need to answer questions from the audience about my organization's recent policy change to rezone the 300 block of downtown.

Attached is a 2-page document with further information about the project. Pretend you are a resident of the downtown area who is angry about the proposal. Please ask a question and engage with me in a back-and-forth dialogue. Afterwards, please provide constructive feedback on how I answered."

You can steer it to act as, for example, an audience member at a town hall, a skeptical reporter, or a tough interviewer. With a clearly defined role and context, it can engage in dialogue, ask probing questions, and offer structured feedback on your responses. Some platforms also support voice interaction, allowing you to speak with it and receive in-character replies.

*\[Visual description]:* The AI responds to the prompt and then uses the voice interaction feature to speak with the AI.

*\[Visual description]:* Text appears: Disclaimer: The commercial tools mentioned in this course are for illustrative purposes and do not imply endorsement by InnovateUS.

A note of caution: steering shapes tone, perspective, and style, but it does not ensure factual accuracy. Asking it to sound like a professor does not make its answers more reliable. Use steering to guide how the output is framed, and verify important claims independently.

*\[Visual description]:* Text appears: Sound like a professor does not equal think like a professor.

To be clear: GenAI is never a replacement for actual engagement with residents. It can't tell you what people actually think. But it can help you be a better conversation starter.

*\[Visual description]:* Title card: 8: Tutor.

Often, we don't need GenAI to give us answers — we need it to generate questions: for witnesses, for the public, to prepare for a meeting, or to test our understanding of a topic.

*\[Visual description]:* An AI is asked to write a ten-question test to test a user's understanding of Gen-AI.

With the right instructions, GenAI can produce questions and act as a coach or tutor that can help you simplify complicated topics, prepare a study guide and quiz you, and suggest what to study next.

*\[Visual description]:* The user asks the AI to give the questions one by one and to give feedback it complies and begins the test.

*\[Visual description]:* Title card: 9: Coding.

As with text, you can use Generative AI to write and analyze computer code.  These tools can significantly increase the speed at which programmers work.

More importantly, they are lowering the barrier to entry, making it easier for non-experts to start building simple applications.

You may hear this described as “vibe coding,” using natural language to generate working code without deep programming expertise.

*\[Visual description]:* An AI is asked to analyse a piece of code, explain what it does, and to identify bugs, security vulnerabilities, performance issues, and areas where the code can be simplified.

While you might not build a fully functional government system, you can use GenAI to prototype new services and tools.

In this demo, we write a prompt, asking an AI assistant to build a web application for an online renewable energy grant application. The prompt requests a form to collect user information, logic to check eligibility, and storage of submitted applications.

*\[Visual description]:* The AI responds to the prompt in a set of blocks of code separated by both their programming language and utility.

The AI produces a structured block of code – not a finished product, but a solid start that a developer could immediately build on.

GenAI can also review existing code to flag potential security issues, generate documentation, and create test cases. As with any coding task, you still need enough understanding to evaluate and validate the output.

*\[Visual description]:* Title card: 10: Data Analysis.

You can use GenAI to analyze and manipulate data — both text and numbers — making it useful even for those of us who aren't data scientists.

*\[Visual description]:* A complex spreadsheet titled Raw Fictional U.S. Public Service Requests - Deliberately Messy Data is shown and uploaded to an AI.

We upload a ridership spreadsheet and generate a visualization of year-over-year bus trends. Next, we can ask for the right Excel formula to extract phone numbers. Then, we can clean and properly format a list of names into a structured table, catching and fixing missed details like a middle initial.

*\[Visual description]:* The AI analyses the spreadsheet and gives advice regarding the data.

You need to review for errors. But the time saved from using these tools will more than make up for the time spent reviewing and verifying.

*\[Visual description]:* A list titled Ten common tasks is shown, which lists: Drafting, Brainstorming, Editing and Proofreading, Summarizing, Categorizing and Labeling, Synthesizing Information Across Sources, Role-Playing, Coaching and Question Generation, Coding, and Data Analysis.

For every task in this list, GenAI gets you most of the way there, fast. Your expertise gets you the rest of the way. This combination is more powerful than either approach independently.

*\[Visual description]:* Text appears: You plus GenAI is greater than You OR GenAI.

Now that we've covered the ten ways you can use GenAI in your day-to-day work, let’s talk about getting the most from these tools.
