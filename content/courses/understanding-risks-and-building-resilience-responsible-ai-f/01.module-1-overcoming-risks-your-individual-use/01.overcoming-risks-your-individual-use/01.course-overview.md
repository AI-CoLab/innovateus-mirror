# Course Overview

This lesson introduces the module and turns the focus from how to use GenAI toward the risks of misusing it. Participants will gain an understanding of what can go wrong through real-world cases, such as leaked trade secrets and fabricated legal citations. The module's learning goals and key risks—data privacy, hallucinations, and bias—are previewed.
