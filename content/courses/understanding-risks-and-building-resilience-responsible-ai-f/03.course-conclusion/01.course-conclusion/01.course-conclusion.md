Congratulations on completing the Responsible AI for Public Professionals course!

*\[Visual description]:* Title card: What we’ve covered.

We began by demystifying GenAI — providing a clear understanding of what it is and how it can enhance our work in government. We then explored practical applications across ten key areas, from drafting and brainstorming to data analysis and coding. Through hands-on exercises, you mastered the art of prompt engineering.

Recognizing the importance of responsible AI use, we addressed risks — data privacy, hallucinations, and bias — and gave you practical strategies for managing them: continuous learning, critical evaluation, human oversight, transparency, and prioritizing low-risk uses.

We also explored broader societal issues: copyright, deepfakes, astroturfing, and the implications of private ownership.

Our ultimate goal is to place humanity at the heart of technological advancement — AI on tap, not on top — ensuring that AI helps our public sector workers and strengthens our communities.

What's next in your journey?

Visit the InnovateUS homepage to explore more courses and live workshops, learn alongside peers from across the country and around the world, and build a learning pathway tailored to your role and the challenges you're actually working on. Tell us what you want to learn next — this field is moving fast, and so are we.

Every major technology shift rewards the professionals who stay curious, ask hard questions, and keep their values at the center of their work. That's exactly what this course has been preparing you to do. GenAI is a force multiplier for people who think critically and care about their work — and that's you.

Thank you for your dedication to public service. The residents you serve are lucky to have professionals who take both the opportunity and the responsibility seriously.
