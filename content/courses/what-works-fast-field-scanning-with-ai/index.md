**What Works: Fast Field Scanning with AI** is designed to help public professionals accelerate the process of finding and implementing urgently-needed evidence-based solutions to public problems.

The course teaches public professionals methods for researching, evaluating, and identifying what works and determining whether solutions that worked in one jurisdiction will work in their communities.

Comprising eight modules, the course equips learners with skills for getting smarter, faster from data from documents and people, while also providing hands-on advice for using Generative AI (GenAI) to improve the learning process.

Designed for busy professionals, the course will help you scan for the most effective policies, innovations, and interventions and build them with the communities you serve.
