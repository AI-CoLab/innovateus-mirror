# Resources: Evidence clearinghouses

Evidence clearinghouses collect and evaluate academic studies of policy interventions and are one of the most important sources of evidence for finding what works. Here are links to some clearinghouses you may find useful. We also welcome your further suggestions at \<insert InnovateUS email contact>.

### Multi-topic clearinghouses

- [Results First Clearinghouse Database](https://evidence2impact.psu.edu/what-we-do/research-translation-platform/results-first-resources/clearing-house-database/?)

  Established with the support of The Pew Charitable Trusts and now operated by Penn State University, Results First catalogs and rates interventions on a spectrum from negative impact to positive impact. It covers interventions related to education, public and mental health, child and family well-being, employment and job training, crime and delinquency, substance use, and sexual behavior and teen pregnancy.

- [Campbell Collaboration](https://www.campbellcollaboration.org/)

  The Campbell Collaboration is a social science research network which catalogs systematic reviews, develops ['evidence and gap maps'](https://www.campbellcollaboration.org/better-evidence.html) to visually represent the strength of evidence associated with specific interventions. It also produces research summaries and policy briefs. The Collaboration covers topics that include ageing, disability, education, children & young persons wellbeing, social welfare, crime and justice, and business and management.

- [Social Systems Evidence (SSE)](https://www.socialsystemsevidence.org/)

  SSE is an initiative of the McMaster Health Forum and the [Monash Sustainable Development Institute](https://www.monash.edu/msdi) (MSDI). It is a repository of research evidence syntheses related to a range of government sectors and program areas such as: climate action, community and social services, economic development and growth, education, environmental conservation, education, housing and transportation.

### Topic-specific clearinghouses

- [Cochrane Library](https://www.cochranelibrary.com/)

  The Cochrane Library is a long standing and respected source of systematic evidence reviews in medicine and healthcare.

- [Health Systems Evidence (HSE)](https://www.healthsystemsevidence.org/)

  HSE is an initiative of the McMaster Health Forum which provides a repository of research evidence syntheses covering governance, financial and delivery arrangements within health systems.

- [What Works Clearinghouse (WWC)](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc)

  The WWC is an initiative of the Institute of Education Sciences (IES) within the U.S. Department of Education. It aims to provide scientific evidence on which to ground education practice and policy. It provides practice-guides, intervention reports, and reviews of individual studies that address school readiness and education from infancy through adulthood across a range of population groups. The WWC also offers [training](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/GroupDesign5) for those interested in becoming reviewers.

- [Pathways to Work Evidence Clearinghouse](https://www.acf.hhs.gov/opre/project/pathways-work-evidence-clearinghouse)

  The [Pathways to Work Evidence Clearinghouse](http://pathwaystowork.acf.hhs.gov/) focuses on systematic reviews of interventions that improve employment-related outcomes for populations with low incomes. It includes ‘evidence snapshots’, synthesis reports, and a [series of YouTube videos](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLypiJrod4DeiZhNDmmVb9Jh8J99RHvxWm) that assist you to explore their materials.

- [Collaboration for Environmental Evidence Database of Evidence Reviews (CEEDER)](https://environmentalevidence.org/ceeder/)

  CEEDER aims to help policy makers, managers, funders, and the general public to find reliable evidence reviews and syntheses to inform their decision making in environmental management. It catalogs and rates evidence reviews sourced from commercially published journals and gray literature sources.

- [Network for Business Sustainability (NBS)](https://nbs.net/)

  The NBS is a non-profit focused on sustainable development with six ‘expert knowledge hubs’: climate change, circular economy, sustainable finance, sustainable innovation, social justice and sustainable business education. It offers evidence-based content that draws on the research and experience of academics and managers in the network.
