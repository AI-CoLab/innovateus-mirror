# Resources: News Outlets

### Finding solutions from news outlets

News items can often be a way to begin learning about solutions. Solutions or constructive journalism news outlets, in particular, focus specifically on solutions and things that are going right.

#### Solutions Journalism News Outlets

- [Solutions Journalism Network](https://www.solutionsjournalism.org/)
- A key organization dedicated to promoting and providing resources for solutions journalism, offering a database of solutions-focused articles.  That database, the Solutions Story Tracker, pulls, summarizes, tags and links to stories from 2,000 different news outlets.

  [The New York Times - Headway Section](https://www.nytimes.com/international/section/headway)
- A section focusing on exploring what can be done about the biggest challenges facing society and the innovations that aim to move the world forward.

  [The Philadelphia Citizen](https://thephiladelphiacitizen.org/)
- A publication that offers news and solutions-driven stories focused on Philadelphia, aiming to engage citizens in solving the city's challenges.

  [Yes! Magazine](https://www.yesmagazine.org/)
- This publication focuses on optimistic storytelling, covering peace, justice, and sustainability issues with a solutions-oriented approach.

  [Positive News](https://www.positive.news/)
- A magazine and online platform based in the UK that emphasizes constructive journalism, showcasing positive developments around the globe.

  [The Christian Science Monitor](https://www.csmonitor.com/)
- Offers a "[Making a Difference" section](https://www.csmonitor.com/World/Making-a-difference) among its coverage, focusing on solutions and positive outcomes to various challenges.

  [The Upside](https://www.theguardian.com/world/series/the-upside) (from The Guardian)
- Dedicated to positive news, The Guardian's Upside section reports on innovations and solutions addressing global issues.

  [The Hechinger Report](https://hechingerreport.org/)
- Specializes in education, exploring solutions to problems across the education system, from early childhood to higher education.

  [CityLab](https://www.bloomberg.com/citylab)

  Compiles ideas and solutions from around the world that are focused on city-level problems.

#### Topic Searching

- [Google News](https://news.google.com/)
- A news aggregator that enables you to search across a variety of publications.

  [Google Alerts](https://www.google.com/alerts)

  Enables you to receive an email when new results for a topic you have defined shows up in Google Search.
