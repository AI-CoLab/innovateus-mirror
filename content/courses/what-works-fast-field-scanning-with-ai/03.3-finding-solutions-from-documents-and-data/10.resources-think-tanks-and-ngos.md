# Resources: Think tanks and NGOs

###

Finding solutions in academic research Academic research often offers discussions backed by evidence about policy interventions or programs. The challenge is that many journal articles are behind a paywall and can be hard to access without a university login. Here we have listed some aggregators of journal articles that are free to search. We have also included examples of other resources that can similarly lead you to the evidence related to solutions of interest to you.

- [National Governors Association (NGA) Center for Best Practices](https://www.nga.org/bestpractices/)

  The NGA  Center for Best Practices is a research and development firm that directly serves the nation’s Governors. Its programs cover policy areas that include energy, environment, public health, education, children and families, economic policy and infrastructure.

- [Social Science Research Council (SSRC)](https://www.ssrc.org/)

  The SSRC draws on a global network of 250,000 researchers to share evidence-based policy solutions. Content is curated and organized to be accessible to decision-makers through various [knowledge platforms and reports](https://www.ssrc.org/how-we-work/knowledge-platforms-and-reports/). The ‘[Measure of America](https://measureofamerica.org/)’ platform, for example, summarizes data related to the distribution of well-being in the United States.

- [The Urban Institute](https://www.urban.org/)

  Founded by President Johnson in 1968, the Urban Institute seeks to offer solutions through economic and social policy research. Urban uses [stories, data tools and blogs](https://www.urban.org/stories-data-tools-and-blogs) to make its research accessible.

- [Abdul Latif Jameel Poverty Action Lab (J-PAL)](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/)

  With a network of more than 900 researchers at universities around the world, J-PAL conducts randomized impact evaluations to help ensure policy directed at addressing poverty is informed by evidence.

- [Economic Policy Institute (EPI)](https://www.epi.org/)

  EPI is a non-partisan think tank which focuses on the economic conditions confronted by low and middle income Americans. EPI’s [Economic Analysis and Research Network (EARN)](https://earn.us/about/) partners with local and state-based organizations to research and advocate for policies that improve equity.
