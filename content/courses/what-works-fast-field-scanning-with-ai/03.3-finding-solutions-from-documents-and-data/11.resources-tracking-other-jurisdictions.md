# Resources: Tracking other jurisdictions

###

### Finding solutions from other jurisdictions

Organizations that work across various levels of government to promote the adoption and implementation of policies and practices that are grounded in evidence of effectiveness offer many opportunities to find solutions.

- [Results for America (RFA)](https://results4america.org/)

  RFA helps policymakers at all levels of government harness the power of evidence and data to solve the world’s greatest challenges. Their work includes identifying and scaling effective solutions across various policy areas. RFA’s [Economic Mobility Catalog](https://catalog.results4america.org/) focuses on strategies that local leaders can use to improve upward economic mobility for residents.

- [The National Governors Association (NGA)](https://www.nga.org/)

  NGA is a bipartisan organization of U.S. states' governors. The [NGA’s Center for Best Practices](https://www.nga.org/bestpractices/) develops and shares innovative solutions aimed at improving governance at the state level.

- [The Pew Charitable Trusts](https://www.pewtrusts.org/en/)

  Through its [Results First](https://www.pewtrusts.org/en/projects/archived-projects/results-first-initiative) initiative, Pew helped states identify and invest in programs that had proven to be effective in achieving desired outcomes. The [Results First Clearinghouse Database](https://evidence2impact.psu.edu/results-first-resources/clearing-house-database/#:~:text=The%20Results%20First%E2%84%A2%20initiative,on%20this%20resource%20in%202021.) is now operated by Penn State University.

- [What Works Cities (WWC)](https://whatworkscities.bloomberg.org/)

  A Bloomberg Philanthropies initiative, What Works Cities helps local governments use data and evidence effectively to tackle pressing challenges and improve residents' lives.

- [The Coalition for Evidence-Based Policy](https://www.evidencebasedpolicy.org/#:~:text=The%20Coalition%20for%20Evidence%2DBased,administrations%20and%20Congress%20to%20get)

  Now a part of the Laura and John Arnold Foundation, this coalition works to increase government effectiveness through the use of rigorous evidence about "what works."

- [The Center for Evidence-Based Policy](https://centerforevidencebasedpolicy.org/)

  A national leader in evidence-based decision making and policy design, the center works with public sector leaders at the federal, state, and local levels to solve problems, improve outcomes, and achieve goals more effectively.

- [The Government Accountability Office (GAO) - Evidence-Based Policymaking Page](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-105460\&sa=D\&source=docs\&ust=1716471755688987\&usg=AOvVaw3DjPeEs_kB8ft6TgDWQ2a0)

  GAO provides Congress, the heads of executive agencies, and the public with timely, fact-based, non-partisan information that can be used to improve government and address a variety of challenges through evidence-based policymaking.

- [The National Academies ](https://www.nationalacademies.org/)

  The National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine are private, nonprofit institutions that provide expert advice on pressing challenges facing the nation and world. The Academies aim to inform sound policies and public opinion, and advance the pursuit of science, engineering, and medicine.

- [Congressional Research Service](https://crsreports.congress.gov/Home/About)

  The Congressional Research Service (CRS) serves as shared staff to congressional committees and Members of Congress. [CRS Reports](https://crsreports.congress.gov/) provides public access to non-partisan research products produced by the CRS for the United States Congress.
