# Designing Democratic Engagement with AI: Practical Strategies for Public Participation

![](<moodle/courses/46/democratic-engagement-course.png>)

### Welcome!

This course provides public sector professionals with a practical understanding of community engagement and its role in improving public service delivery. Through hands-on activities and best practices, participants learn to design and run engagement as a powerful tool for connecting with the communities they serve. The course covers the fundamentals of engagement, practical approaches across contexts, methods for consultation, deliberation, and co-creation, and strategies for genuinely listening to communities. By the end of the course, participants will be equipped to plan and deliver engagement effectively and confidently in their public sector roles.
