# Video 7 – Designing Incentives for Participation

#### Video 7: Designing Incentives for Participation

This video distinguishes between intrinsic motivators — such as civic purpose and visible impact — and extrinsic rewards, and explains why the strongest incentive is showing people how their input will be used. It also covers how to reduce barriers through practical support and how AI can reinforce incentives by making contributions visible in real time.
