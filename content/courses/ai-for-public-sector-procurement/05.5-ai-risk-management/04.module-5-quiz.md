# Module 5 Quiz

::knowledge-check{kind="one"}
Your jurisdiction uses an AI tool to monitor contract compliance and match invoices to contract terms. The system suddenly flags a vendor that has had a clean record for two years as having multiple discrepancies. What is the most appropriate response?

- [ ] Accept the AI's assessment and immediately issue a notice of non-compliance to the vendor
- [ ] Dismiss the flags since the vendor has a strong track record and the AI may be malfunctioning
- [x] Investigate the flagged discrepancies using the underlying contract documents and invoice records before taking any action
- [ ] Switch to a different AI tool to see if it produces the same result
::

::knowledge-check{kind="one"}
A staff member wants to use a free AI tool they found online to analyze a contract containing sensitive vendor pricing data. What should they do first?

- [ ] Proceed, since the tool produces accurate results in their testing
- [ ] Ask a colleague to review the output to catch any errors
- [ ] Use the tool but remove the vendor names before uploading
- [x] Check whether the tool is on their jurisdiction's approved list and complies with data security requirements
::

::knowledge-check{kind="several"}
A jurisdiction is rolling out a new AI tool and several staff members have expressed concerns about job displacement. Which of the following are appropriate responses from procurement leadership? Select all that apply.

- [ ] Reassure staff that their concerns are unfounded and move forward without addressing them further
- [x] Communicate clearly and early about what the tool is, what it isn't, and what problems it aims to solve
- [ ] Pause the rollout indefinitely until all staff concerns are fully resolved
- [x] Provide structured training and ongoing learning resources so staff feel confident using the tool
- [ ] Limit access to the tool to staff who are already comfortable with AI
- [x] Create space for staff to raise concerns and give feedback throughout the rollout
- [x] Emphasize that AI will handle repetitive tasks, freeing staff to focus on higher-value work that requires their expertise
::

Which of the following best describes the goal of cross-functional collaboration in AI risk management?

- [ ] To slow down AI adoption until all risks are fully understood
- [ ] To transfer responsibility for AI risk from procurement to IT
- [x] To bring procurement, IT, legal, and leadership together to assess risks and define mitigation strategies before deployment
- [ ] To create a committee that approves every AI output before it is used
::
