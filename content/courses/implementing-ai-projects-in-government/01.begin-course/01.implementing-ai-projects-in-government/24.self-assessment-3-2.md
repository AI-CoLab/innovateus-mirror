::knowledge-check{kind="several"}
According to the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, which of the following are characteristics of accountable and transparent AI? (Select all that apply)

- [x] How an AI decision system is used should be made transparent to users
- [ ] It should be clear who is responsible for the outcomes of using the AI system
- [ ] The AI system should be able to explain its decision-making process in detail
- [x] The AI system should be able to make decisions without human oversight

:::feedback{when="incorrect"}
According to the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, the characteristics of accountable and transparent AI are that how an AI decision system is used should be made transparent to users, and it should be clear who is responsible for the outcomes of using the system. Explainability is a separate characteristic, and accountability and transparency do not imply that AI systems should make decisions without human oversight.
:::
