# Video 1.3: Four common causes

This video discusses four common causes of data quality issues in public sector organizations: poor validation due to outdated systems, lack of formal data governance programs, cultural factors, and lack of funding. It provides examples of how these factors can impact data quality and offers practical solutions, such as prioritizing data validation capabilities when procuring new systems, establishing formal data governance processes, bridging cultural divides between IT and program units, and dedicating sufficient funding and resources for data quality efforts.
