::knowledge-check{kind="one"}
In the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, which function focuses on establishing the ecosystem for the other functions to succeed?

- [x] Govern
- [ ] Map

  Map involves activities to identify specific risks for a particular AI project, not establishing the ecosystem for other functions
- [ ] Measure

  Measure involves activities to measure the level of identified risks, not establishing the ecosystem for other functions
- [ ] Manage

  Manage involves activities to develop and implement strategies to mitigate measured risks, not establishing the ecosystem for other functions
::
