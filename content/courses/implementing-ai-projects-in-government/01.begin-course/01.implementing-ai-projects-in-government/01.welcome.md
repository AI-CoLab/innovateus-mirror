# Welcome!

## Overview

This course will address many of the challenges inherent in expanding AI throughout your organization, including an overview of the skills and talent needed to implement these technologies and a discussion of risk management, data quality, and related ethical issues. We will focus on the talent needs for your organization and evaluate different strategies to attract and train talent. We will also learn the importance of AI risk management and assess the unique risks posed by Generative AI.

If you haven’t yet taken the first course in this series, *Designing AI Projects in Government*, we highly recommend you do so,  as it focuses on understanding different types of AI and identifying the right AI projects for your organization.\
