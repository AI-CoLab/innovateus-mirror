# Video 2.4: Training and recruiting talent

This video discusses three main avenues for developing an AI-ready workforce: training existing staff, hiring new talent, and seeking outside assistance. It explains the advantages and considerations associated with each approach. The video also emphasizes the importance of creating roles and structures that signal to data scientists that they will be doing meaningful work and the need to prioritize diversity and rigorously evaluate skills when building an AI team.
