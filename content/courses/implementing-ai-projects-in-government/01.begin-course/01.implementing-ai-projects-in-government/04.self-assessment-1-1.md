When considering an AI project, a department should:

- [x] Assume their data is too messy or incomplete and not pursue the project

  Departments should assess data fitness for the specific purpose before deciding not to pursue an AI project.
- [ ] Assess the fitness of their data for the specific purpose of the AI project

  The correct answer is B. Departments should assess the fitness of their data for the specific purpose of the AI project before deciding whether to proceed.
- [ ] Proceed with the AI project regardless of data quality concerns

  Proceeding without assessing data quality can lead to inaccurate AI outputs and unintended consequences.
- [x] Wait until they have perfect data before considering any AI projects

  Perfect data is not necessary; data should be assessed for fitness for the specific purpose.
