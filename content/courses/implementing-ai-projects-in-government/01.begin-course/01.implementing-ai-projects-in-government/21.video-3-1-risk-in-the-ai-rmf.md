# Video 3.1: Risk in the AI RMF

This video introduces the critical importance of robust AI risk management and the role of the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) in ensuring responsible and safe AI deployment. It explains how the AI RMF is a flexible guide to help organizations identify, assess, and manage AI risks in a manner appropriate to their specific contexts and risk tolerances.
