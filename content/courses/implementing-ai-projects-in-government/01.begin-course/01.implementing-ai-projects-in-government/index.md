# Implementing AI Projects in Government

#### Begin Course!

This course will show you how to expand and deepen AI work in your organization. We will learn about different types of AI and how it can support high-priority goals and projects. The lessons from this course will allow you to use these exciting new tools as a powerful companion to assist you in your work. By the end of this course, you will understand the different types of AI and their applications in government, and know-how to identify and prioritize high-impact AI projects. Whether you're exploring AI for the first time or looking to deepen your knowledge, this course will provide you with a solid foundation to confidently lead your organization into the age of AI.
